The claimant explained that she did not know that the decision had been made until benefit payments to her bank account stopped when the previous award expired. She said that she had not received the decision notice. The DLA file shows that she had phoned up a week before the appeal was made to ask when a decision would be made on her renewal claim. The DM accepts that she had not received the decision notice, and admits the late appeal. "
