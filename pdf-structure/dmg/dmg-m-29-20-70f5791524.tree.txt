        "1. A new immigration status for frontier workers has been introduced in compliance with the UK’s obligations under the Withdrawal Agreement1. The status has been introduced via the Citizens’ Rights (Frontier Workers) (EU Exit) Regulations 20202 (“The Frontier Workers SI”). These regulations came into force on 4.11.203, except4 Part 2 of the SI (with the exception of regulations 6(1)(b) & 6(2) - see paragraph 2); Part 4; regulation 24 (in so far as it relates to an appeal against a decision made under Part 2 or Part 4), and Regulation 28, which come into force at 11pm on 31.12.20.  "

    "Background "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "3. The purpose of this Frontier Workers SI is to protect the rights of EEA nationals who, as workers1 or self-employed persons2, have pursued an economic activity as a frontier worker in the UK by the end of the transition period on 31.12.20 and wish to continue to do so thereafter (Part 2 of the SI). They have rights under the Withdrawal Agreement, the EEA European Free Trade Association (EFTA) Separation Agreement and the Swiss Citizens’ Rights Agreement (referred to collectively throughout this Memo as “the Agreements”) to continue "
        "to enter the UK from 11pm on 31.12.20 and work as frontier workers for as long as they continue to be so. "
  P (block)
    "1 TFEU, Art 45; 2 Art 49 "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "4. Frontier workers’ rights are granted under specified legislation1. Those regulations will continue to apply to all EEA workers covered under the Withdrawal Agreement. This means that frontier workers are entitled to access benefits in the same manner as EEA workers subject to the Citizens Directive 2004/38 currently do.   "
  P (block)
    "1 Regulation (EU) 492/11 "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "5. The Agreements1 require that a frontier worker document scheme is to be implemented by the end of the transition period. In addition, the UK has decided (as a matter of domestic policy) to give effect to the Agreements, for a frontier worker to hold a permit2 which certifies their right to enter the UK as a protected frontier worker from 1.7.21. This Frontier Workers SI establishes a frontier workers’ permit scheme (Part 3 of the SI) under which a protected frontier worker can apply for and be issued with a permit certifying their rights to continue to enter and work in the UK (please see paragraphs 42 - 51 for further guidance on permits). "
  P (block)

  L (block)
    LI (block)

This Frontier Workers SI also sets out the circumstances in which a protected frontier worker’s rights to enter the UK as a frontier worker can be restricted, and a permit can be refused or revoked (Part 4 of the SI), in accordance with the Agreements. It also provides protected frontier workers with statutory rights of appeal, and with a right of administrative review, against certain decisions to restrict their rights (Part 5 of the SI). "
