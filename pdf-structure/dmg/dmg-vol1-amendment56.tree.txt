    "Writing the response to the First-tier Tribunal  "
  P (block)
    "06330 The main purpose of the written appeal response is to provide the FtT and the claimant with a comprehensive explanation of the reasons for the DM’s decision. DWP will provide a response within 28 calendar days after the receipt of the appeal papers from HMCTS1. It must always contain2 "
  P (block)
    "1. the name and address of the DM who made the decision against which the claimant is appealing "
  P (block)
    "2. the name and address of the presenting officer, if one is going to attend the hearing "
  P (block)
    "3. the name and address of the relevant DWP office where documents can be sent "
  P (block)
    "4. the name and addresses of any other respondents and their representatives, if known "
  P (block)
    "5. whether the Secretary of State opposes the appeal and if so the grounds for that opposition if this is not already set out in any documents the FtT has. "
  P (block)
    "6. any further information that the FtT issues a direction for. "
  P (block)
    "1 TP (FtT) (SEC) Rules, rule 24(1)(c); 2 rule 24(2) "
  P (block)
    "06331  The response should  "
  P (block)
    "1. focus on the circumstances that existed at the time that the appealed decision was made and  "
  P (block)
    "2. deal solely with the issues raised by the appeal.  "
  P (block)
    "06332  The response writer should adopt the role of friend of the court1. This means that the response should  "
  P (block)
    "1. give proper emphasis to points in the claimant’s favour and "
  P (block)
    "2. deal with any unresolved points put forward by the appellant. Account should be taken of these even if they are, in the response writer’s opinion, only vaguely relevant to the question at issue.  "
  P (block)
    "1 R(I) 4/65, Appendix "
  P (block)
    "06333 Along with the appeal response, the DM must also provide "
  P (block)
    "1. a copy of any written record of the decision and any statement of reasons for that decision, if they were not sent with the appellants notice of appeal "
  P (block)
    "2. copies of all other relevant documents that the Secretary of State holds, including all relevant documents and information that set out the decision making history and chronology. Where relevant documents are not in the "
  P (block)
    " DM’s possession, the DM should make the FtT aware in the appeal response of why they cannot provide the documents1. "
  P (block)
    "3. a copy of the notice of appeal, any documents provided by the appellant with the notice of appeal and the name and address of any representative the appellant may have2. "
  P (block)
    "1 [2016] AACR 24 (FN v SSWP (ESA) [2015] UKUT 670 (AAC); 2 TP (FtT) (SEC) Rules, rule 24(4) "
  P (block)
    "06334 Unless the FtT has made an order prohibiting the disclosure of certain documents1 (see DMG 06287 et seq), the DM must provide a copy of the appeal response and any other papers to each other party to the appeal. If the party has a representative then they must be provided with a copy of any papers and therefore there is no need to provide them to the party2. If they wish, the appellant can then make a written submission or supply other documents in reply to the DM’s appeal response3. "
  P (block)
    "1 TP (FtT) (SEC) Rules, rule 14; 2 rule 11(6); 3 rule 24(5)-(7) "
  H1 (block)
    "Recommendation to the First-tier Tribunal "
  P (block)
    "06335 In order to assist the FtT to take the most appropriate course of action, the response to the FtT should indicate whether, if the appeal succeeds on the issue raised, there are other issues which require determination. If so, the response should also state whether the Secretary of State considers that the FtT should deal with them, or whether they should decide the issue under appeal and refer the case to the DM for a final outcome decision to be made. "
  H1 (block)
    "Outcome decision required "
  P (block)
    "06336 The following examples are where the response writer may wish to request that the FtT give an outcome decision. "
  P (block)
    " Note: If the FtT does not accept the recommendation, the DM must comply with the FtT’s directions. "
  H1 (block)
    " Example 1 "
  P (block)
    " The DM decides that a claim for IS is disallowed because the claimant has no right to reside in the UK. The claim form has given sufficient information to decide all other conditions of entitlement. The response requests the FtT to give an outcome decision on entitlement if the appeal on the issue of the right to reside is allowed. "
  H1 (block)
    " Example 2 "
  P (block)
    " The DM disallows a claim for IIDB on the grounds that the claimant is not suffering from a prescribed disease. The response requests the FtT to assess the degree of disablement if the appeal on the issue of diagnosis is allowed. "

  P (block)
    "06700  An appeal against a decision of the UT on a question of law should be made to the Court of Appeal or, in Scotland, the Court of Session1. All action on appeals to the Courts will be taken by DMA Leeds. "
  P (block)
    "1 SS Act 98, s 15  "
  H1 (block)
    "Who may apply for leave  "
  P (block)
    "06701  An application for leave to appeal from a decision of the UT may be made by  "
  P (block)
    "1. any person who was entitled to appeal against the FtT’s decision  "
  P (block)
    "2. any other person who was a party to the FtT proceedings1.  "
  P (block)
    "1 SS Act 98, s 15(3)  "

  P (block)

  P (block)
