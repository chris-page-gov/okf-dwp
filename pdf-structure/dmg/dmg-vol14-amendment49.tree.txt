          "Jason and Sarah are a couple in receipt of SPC. Jason has a pension pot of £50,000 which he doesn’t wish to access at the moment but might do at a later stage. The DM determines that as an annuity income, this would produce £2,500 per annum or £48.07 per week. This is based on 100% of the rate of annuity that the fund would generate. This amount is taken into account as a notional income and is deducted from the award of SPC. Jason and Sarah then later decide to put the £50,000 into a flexi draw down fund and take an actual income of £50 per week. The DM now decides that the amount of income to take into account each week is £50. There is no notional amount to take into account. "
