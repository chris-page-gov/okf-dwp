The Order comes into force on 9.11.201.   "

    "BACKGROUND "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "2. The Scottish Government has introduced a system of Scottish Child Payments. Initially, payment will be £10.00 per week to low income families for each eligible child, with no cap on the number of children in families. Payments will be monthly. Applications, payments and disputes will be administered by Social Security Scotland. "
  P (block)
    "TREATMENT OF PAYMENTS FOR SPC "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "3. Scottish Child Payments are not prescribed as income for the purposes of the assessment of SPC1. Scottish Child Payments are disregarded as capital for the purpose of calculation income for SPC2. "
