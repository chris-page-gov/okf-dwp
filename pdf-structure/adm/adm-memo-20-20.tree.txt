    "INTRODUCTION "
  P (block)
    "1 The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care has announced a scheme of payments for people on low incomes in areas with a high incidence of Coronavirus, who need to self-isolate and are unable to work from home. Applications, payments and disputes are the responsibility of Local Authorities. The scheme started on 1.9.20.  "
  P (block)
    " ELIGIBILITY "
  L (block)
    LI (block)
      LBody (block)
        "2 Eligibility is restricted to people who are resident in Blackburn with Darwen, Oldham or Pendle and who meet the following criteria: "
  P (block)
    "1.  They have received notification from NHS Test and Trace; "
  P (block)
    "2. They have agreed to comply with the notification from NHS Test and Trace and provided contact details to their LA; "
  P (block)
    "3. They are employed or self-employed; "
  P (block)
    "4.  They are unable to work from home and will lose income as a result. "
  P (block)
    "5.  They are currently receiving UC, WTC, income-related ESA, income-based JSA, IS or SPC or HB. "
  P (block)
    "Virus infection rates are monitored and the scope of the scheme may be subject to change in the future.  "
