    "EARNINGS THRESHOLD CALCULATION APPLIED TO GRACE PERIOD  "
  P (block)
    "2. For households where the benefit cap is applied through Universal Credit there can be a period of 9 months, known as a grace period, during which time their benefit may not be capped. "
  P (block)
    "3.  To qualify for a grace period, the household earnings must have been at least the relevant earnings threshold for the previous 12 months/assessment periods. "
  P (block)
    "4.  In UC, when the earnings threshold has been met, the benefit cap grace period is to be calculated using the national living wage as at the beginning of each month or assessment period in which the claimant was working. "
  P (block)
    "5.  As the amount of the national living wage changes each year, this clarification will ensure that the correct amount of national living wage, relating to the time when the work was undertaken, is used to calculate whether a grace period "
