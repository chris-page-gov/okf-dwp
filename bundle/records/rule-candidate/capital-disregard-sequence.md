# Capital disregard: evidence and decision sequence candidate

**Review state: unreviewed. Executable: no.**

## Proposition to review

The captured guidance separates whether an item can be disregarded, the evidence for it and its duration. DMG 84355 places the disregard decision before valuation and before deemed weekly income is calculated. These are source-bound propositions, not findings about a claimant or an assurance of current law.

## Proposed review sequence

1. Identify the asset, relevant ownership, benefit and decision date.
2. Identify the applicable disregard category and supporting evidence.
3. Review the legal basis, amendments, commencement, territorial scope, DMG Memo 11/20 and any applicable decisions.
4. Have a domain reviewer determine whether the disregard and period are supported; record missing or disputed evidence.
5. Only then assess how valuation and deemed income should be represented for the agreed rule family.

Do not infer that an unanswered field proves ineligibility. Do not apply the Pension Credit sequence to Universal Credit through name similarity.

## Still required before an executable rule

Named policy and legal reviewers; the missing original stakeholder plan; acquired and checked provisions and memo; exceptions; effective dates; evidence/fraud/error requirements; review and appeal paths; synthetic positive, negative and boundary cases; independently accepted expected outcomes. A model-generated expected answer is not a gold standard.
