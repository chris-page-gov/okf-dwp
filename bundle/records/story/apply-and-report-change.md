# Explore application and change journeys

Proposed journey J05, addressing owner request OW02. Evaluate two separate variants: an initial application and a report of a changed household circumstance with an event date.

Acceptance: retain different triggers and completion criteria, accessible explanations, known gaps and a verified official hand-off. Official service steps and operational evidence requirements need further discovery; concept links are starting points only.
