# Review an evidence-backed rule candidate

Proposed journey J02, addressing staff need ST02 and evaluating model proposal MP01. Inspect one bounded capital-related candidate, retaining any missing stages as gaps.

Acceptance: identify supporting source bytes, assumptions, conditions, exceptions, temporal scope, proposed rule and independent synthetic tests. Record review status separately from successful parsing or retrieval.

Negative controls include a changed source hash and an incorrectly reused historical rate. Policy and engineering review are required before executable use. This story is not an entitlement algorithm.
