# Support accessible source discovery

Proposed journey J06, extending the owner's customer-journey request. Help a person find a source page, understand the unofficial record and recognise damaged or missing extracted text.

Acceptance: observe keyboard completion, focus order, link purpose, reading support and access to the original. Record the actual browser and assistive technology used. A tool-generated page snapshot alone does not demonstrate accessibility.

Where extraction is unclear, identify the original-source route and need for human support. Accessibility specialists and people who use assistive technology must validate practical task completion.
