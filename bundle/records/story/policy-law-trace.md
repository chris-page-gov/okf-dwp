# Trace guidance and legal references

Proposed journey J01, addressing staff need ST01 and owner request OW01. Start with the capital-disregard entry point and distinguish captured DWP guidance from legal references and project-authored interpretation.

Acceptance: retain original page links, capture dates, source types and unresolved memo or legislation dependencies. A legal link must disclose whether it identifies a work, provision or verified dated version.

CPAG's captured navigation supplies no substantive interpretation. A policy specialist and legal reviewer must assess interpretation and applicability before any review status is advanced.
