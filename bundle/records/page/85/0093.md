85425 It is unlikely that there will be direct evidence that a deprivation was for the purpose of getting
benefit. Decide on a person’s reasons for disposing of an income after considering all the facts of the
case. These may include


1. the person’s explanations


2. the timing of the disposal


3. the claimant’s knowledge of the benefit system


4. the likelihood of a benefit claim at the time of the deprivation.




85426 When considering claimant’s intentions in depriving themselves of income the DM should
consider what account of the claimant’s intentions best explains the facts of the case. If the best
explanation of the deprivation is that a significant reason for the disposal was getting or increasing SPC
the DM should calculate a notional income.




Timing of the disposal

85427 DMs should carefully consider the timing of the disposal of income as


1. a claim for SPC made shortly after the disposal of an income may indicate that getting or increasing
SPC was a significant reason for the disposal


2. a claim for SPC made a long time after the disposal of an income is less likely to indicate that getting or
increasing SPC was a significant reason for the disposal. But if there is evidence that a claimant was
considering claiming SPC at a later date, deprivation may still have been for getting or increasing benefit.
