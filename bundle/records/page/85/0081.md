Cost of conversion of foreign money

85335 The DM should fully disregard any


1. bank charges or


2. commission


paid when income paid in foreign currency is converted to sterling 1.


                                                                           1 SPC Regs, Sch IV, para 16


Example


Klaus claims SPC


He is retired and receives a pension of 500 Euros a month from his former employer in Germany. When
Klaus’s bank convert the payment into sterling they charge a commission of £2. The DM disregards the
£2 commission from the amount that Klaus receives in sterling.
