Discretionary trusts

What is a discretionary trust
85245 If the claimant is a beneficiary under a discretionary trust he cannot demand payment under the
terms of the Trust. The Trustees have an absolute discretion as to whether or not to make payments to
the claimant.




Disregard
85246 Disregard1 the first £20 of a payment made to a claimant from a discretionary trust for one or
more of the following items


1. food


2. ordinary clothing or footwear


3. household fuel


4. rent
