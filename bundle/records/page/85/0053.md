Armed Forces and Reserve Forces Compensation Scheme
85211 The Armed Forces and Reserve Forces Compensation Scheme replaces the War Pensions
Scheme for those who suffer injury, ill health or death due to service in the Armed Forces from 6.4.05.


Note: The War Pensions Scheme will continue to run parallel to the new scheme for existing
beneficiaries and for those who make a new claim for a War Pension Scheme payment for a date prior to
6.4.05.
