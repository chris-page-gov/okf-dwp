What is notional income

85400 A person may be treated as having an income that he does not actually have 1. This type of
income is known as notional income and may take different forms.


                                                                                   1 SPC Act 02, s 15(6)(a)



Types of notional income

85401 Notional income may be attributed because of 1


1. deprivation of income (DMG 85408 - 85426)


2. retirement pension income available but not applied for (DMG 85440 - 85478) and


3. income for the claimant paid to a third party (DMG 85500 - 85506).


                                                                                       1 SPC Regs, reg 18



Actual and notional income

85402 A person may have both actual and notional income. DMs need not consider notional income if a
person’s actual income on its own is greater than the claimant’s AMG. If actual income is less than the
AMG the DM should consider if there is notional income.
