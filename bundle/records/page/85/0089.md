Deferral of retirement pension entitlement

85409 From April 2005 pensioners can choose to be paid either an increased pension or a lump sum
payment where they have deferred their entitlement to RP, shared additional pension or graduated
retirement pension. Deferral has to be for at least twelve months (after 6.4.05) for the lump sum option
to be available. Where someone dies whilst deferring, the surviving spouse or civil partner will have the
same choice in relation to the inherited increase or lump sum.
