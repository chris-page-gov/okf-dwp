# DMG Vol 14 Ch 85: Income other than earnings  — PDF page 17

```text
Example


Les has just retired from work. He will be entitled to RP of £97.65 a week. It will be paid fortnightly in
arrears on a Thursday. RP was awarded to Les from 12.4.10 with the first payment actually due to be paid
to him on 22.4.10. The first payment will cover the period 16.4.10 to 22.4.10.


On 12.4.10 Les also makes a claim for SPC. His first payment of SPC is due to be paid to him on 22.4.10
and will cover the period 16.4.10 to 22.4.10 (see DMG 79012).


The DM decides that for the purposes of SPC, Les’s RP of £97.65 has to be taken into account with a
treat as paid date of 22.4.10 because this is the last day of the SPC benefit week in which the RP is
payable.




Questions on the treatment of income

85042 The DM should make further enquiries if there is any doubt whether income fits into any of the
definitions in this Chapter. Enquiries will usually be to the paying authority and should be done before any
decision is made.
```
