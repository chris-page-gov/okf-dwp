Bereavement support payment
85295 The BSP replaces BB for people whose spouse or civil partner dies on or after 6.4.17 1. There are
two rates of BSP; the higher rate and the standard rate. The regular monthly BSP payment is treated as

income other than earnings2 and is disregarded for the period in respect of which it is paid. Any unspent
BSP remaining at the end of this month will become capital (with no disregard applied). The regular
monthly payment does not include any arrears or the higher or standard rate of BSP that may be payable

for the first month of the BSP period3.


Note: See DMG chapter 84 for how BSP affects capital and DMG chapter 59 for more information on
BSP.
