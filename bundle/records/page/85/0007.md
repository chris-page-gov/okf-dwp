85015 The DM should take into account the gross amount of an income. Disregard the sums in DMG
85021 - 85023 and 85200 et seq. when calculating gross income. For SS benefits consider the
guidance at DMG 85095. No disregards should be made for any other expenses or deductions.


Note: For guidance on how to calculate a weekly amount see DMG 85030.



Income of the claimant’s partner

85016 In a claim for SPC, calculate income of the claimant’s partner in the same way as for the claimant 1.

The income should then be treated as the claimant’s income 2.


Note: In SPC a member of a couple is referred to as a partner and both members are referred to as

partners3. DMG 77117.6 gives guidance that a person subject to immigration control should be treated
as not being a member of the same household. The income of a partner subject to immigration control
should not be treated as the claimant's income.
