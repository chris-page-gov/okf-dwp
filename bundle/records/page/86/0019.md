86047 The expense must be incurred in direct connection with the employer's trade or business 1. If
there is some element of private use, for example telephone bills, that part of the bill for business use
should be allowed. Any decision by HMRC on the apportionment of expenses may be taken into account

as evidence. If there is no doubt, that decision can normally be followed 2.


      1 Davies v. Gwaun Cae Gurwen Colliery (1924) 2K8 651; Borley v. Ockended (1925) 2K8 325; 2 R(IS)

86048 An expense that is in the employee's own interest or benefit, or which merely enables the

employee to go to work, would not satisfy the test in DMG 86044. Child minding expenses 1, and the cost
of travel to a single place of work, are examples of expenses that would not satisfy the test.
