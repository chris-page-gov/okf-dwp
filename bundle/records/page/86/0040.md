General

86150 Any earnings derived from employment which ended before the day that the claimant first

satisfies the conditions of entitlement to an award of SPC should be disregarded in full 1. But this
disregard does not apply to the payments described at DMG 86062.The disregard can apply to
successive awards of SPC.


                                                                                  1 SPC Regs, Sch VI, para 6


Example


Charles’ employment ends on 1.6.15. He claims and is entitled to an award of SPC from 2.6.15. The DM
can apply the disregard in DMG 86150 to earnings derived from that employment. At a later stage
Charles’ award of SPC comes to an end because he starts a new job and his earnings exceed the AMG.
After a few months, this second employment also comes to an end and Charles reclaims SPC for a
second time. The DM can apply the disregard in DMG 86150 to earnings derived from this second
employment which has now just ended.


86151 Employees may be entitled to certain payments when their employment ends. These include


1. PILOR (see DMG 86153)


2. PILON (see DMG 86156)


3. holiday pay (see DMG 86070 and 86160)


4. statutory redundancy payments (see DMG 86162).
