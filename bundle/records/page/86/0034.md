Application to claimant and partner

86110 Net earnings should be taken into account less any disregard. The amount of disregard will
depend on


1. whether the earnings are from work in one of the occupations listed at DMG 86113


2. whether the claimant is single


3. who the earnings belong to.


86111 Disregards apply equally to the claimant and partner. The amount of the claimant's or partner's

earnings to disregard is normally1


1. £5 in the case of a claimant with no partner or


2. £10 in the case of a claimant who has a partner or


3. £20 where the claimant or partner satisfy the conditions in DMG 86113 - 86120.
