Introduction 86028 - 86030



86028 This part deals with the calculation and treatment of payments made to SPC claimants who are
employed earners. These will usually be earnings paid by an employer, but may be other types of
payment.


86029 How payments made to employees affect a claim to SPC will depend on


1. whether the work is continuing or


2. whether the work has ended.


86030 Earnings from employment as an employed earner which is ongoing whilst the claimant is in
receipt of SPC are taken into account, less any appropriate earnings disregard, in the same way as other
income.
