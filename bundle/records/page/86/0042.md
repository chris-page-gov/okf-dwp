Work ends whilst SPC is in payment
86159 If the work ends whilst the claimant is in receipt of SPC any PILON should be treated as earnings
and taken into account in the normal way.




Holiday pay

86160 If holiday pay is payable in respect of work which ends before the date on which the claimant

becomes entitled to SPC, it should be disregarded1.


                                                                              1 SPC Regs, Sch VI, para 6


86161 Holiday pay in respect of employment carried out whilst SPC is in payment should be treated as
earnings and be taken into account in the usual way.




Statutory redundancy payments

86162 Employees may receive statutory redundancy payments on termination of their employment.
Such payments should be taken into account as capital.
