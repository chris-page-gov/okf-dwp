Shared lives scheme
78080 A registered shared lives carer provides support and accommodation, in their own home, for a
disabled claimant. The claimant is able to keep their disability benefits (DLA/PIP/ADP) and pays the carer
a weekly contribution, the LA also pay the carer a fixed amount. The claimant will have a lodger/licence
agreement and as such be eligible to obtain HB. In these circumstances the carers will be ignored as non-
dependants (because they are a person who the claimant is liable to make payments on a commercial
basis to) and the claimant will be entitled to the EASD.


Note: The claimant will not be entitled to the EASD if there are people other than the carer, members of
the carer’s household, other shared lives residents or anyone liable to make payments for

accommodation to the carer on a commercial basis in the dwelling who cannot be ignored.1
