General

78120 In certain cases an additional amount may be included in the AMG if a claimant transfers from IS,

JSA(IB) or ESA(IR) to SPC1. This additional amount is known as the transitional amount and it


1. protects the level of a claimant’s benefit income


2. is worked out on an individual basis


3. varies depending on the circumstances of each individual case.


                                              1 SPC Act 02, s 2(3)(b); SPC Regs, reg 6(6)(b) & Sch I, para 6


78121 DMs should note that a transitional amount is not payable 1 in the case of


1. prisoners and


2. members of religious orders who are fully maintained by their order.


                                                                                   1 SPC Regs, reg 6(2), (3)(b)
