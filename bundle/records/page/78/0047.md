Liability for housing costs 78215 - 78219



78215 A person is liable to meet housing costs where the


1. liability falls on them or their partner, except where the liability is to another member of the same

household1 or


2. person liable for those costs is not meeting them and


      2.1 the claimant has to meet the costs to keep on living in the home and


      2.2 it is reasonable in all the circumstances to treat the claimant as liable for the costs 2 or


3. costs are shared with other members of the household who are not close relatives (see DMG 77101) of
the claimant or the claimant’s partner and


      3.1 at least one member of the household is liable for those costs and


      3.2 it is reasonable in the circumstances to treat the claimant as sharing responsibility 3.


       1 SPC Regs, Sch II, para 3(a); 2 Sch II, para 3(b); R(IS) 12/94; R(IS) 8/01; 3 SPC Regs, Sch II, para 3(c)
