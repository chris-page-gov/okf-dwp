Rates from 8.4.19

Deductions apply where



1.   non-dependants aged 18 or over are in remunerative work - £100.65



2.   the DM is satisfied that the non-dependant in remunerative work has a gross weekly income of


2.1 Less than £143.00    £15.60


2.2 £143.00 to £208.99 £35.85


2.3 £209.00 to £271.99 £49.20


2.4 £272.00 to £362.99 £80.55


2.5 £363.00 to £450.99 £91.70


2.6 £451.00 or more      £100.65




3.   any other non-dependant aged 18 or over for whom deductions are relevant - £15.60.
