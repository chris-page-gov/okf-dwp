People admitted to hospital from care homes

78710 In SPC there is no difference between the treatment of people in care homes and those living in
their own homes (other than the deemed weekly income from capital limit). So when a person is admitted
to hospital from a care home, the amount of any reduction in a claimant’s SPC will depend on


1. the period for which the person receives, or is treated as receiving, free in-patient treatment (see DMG
78795 and 78840 et seq) and


2. whether the claimant has a partner who is a member of their household (see DMG 77128 et seq).
