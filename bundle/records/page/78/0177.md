Example 5 - Partner becomes a prisoner

Bernard is aged 68 and lives with his partner Agnes. He is entitled to RP of £127.25 and occupational
pension of £32 a week. He does not qualify for any additional amounts and is not entitled to GC but gets
SC of £20.22 a week. So his total weekly income is £179.45. Agnes is detained in custody and as a result
Bernard’s RP reduces to £79.60. The DM revises the SPC award to remove Agnes from the calculation.


Calculation of GC:


SMG (claimant with no partner) £105.45


Additional amounts               Nil

Amount of GC                     Nil
