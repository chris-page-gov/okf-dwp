Steps to follow when deciding housing costs

78210 When deciding whether an additional amount for housing costs is applicable and if so, what that
amount should be, the DM should determine


1. if the claimant or partner is liable for the housing costs (see DMG 78215)


2. if the amount spent is on the dwelling occupied as the home (see DMG 78188 and 78220)


3. if the housing costs cannot be met (see DMG 78270)


4. how to apportion the housing costs if the dwelling is used for domestic and business use (see DMG
78310)


5. if there are any eligible housing costs that can be allowed (see DMG 78438 et seq)


6. if a deduction for a non-dependant is necessary (see DMG 78500).
