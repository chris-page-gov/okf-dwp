Meaning of person subject to immigration control
78941 PSICs means1 a person who is not a national of the EEA who


1. requires leave to enter or remain in the UK but does not have it or


2. has leave subject to the condition that they have no recourse to public funds or


3. is a sponsored immigrant or


4. has had limited leave extended only because they have appealed a decision to vary, or refuse to vary
that leave.
