Note: The loan payments offer date is the day on which the loan agreement is sent to the claimant 3.


Example:


William is in receipt of IS that includes owner-occupier payments, his benefit week ends each
Wednesday. The transitional end day is Thursday 12 April (the day after the first benefit week that ends
after 6.4.18). From this date William is no longer entitled to mortgage payments but he will get loan
payments from this date (because he had returned all the appropriate signed documents).
