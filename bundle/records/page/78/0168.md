Absent without good cause

128 “Good cause” is for the DM to determine. The onus is on the claimant to show that good cause exists.
Whether or not the employer has authorized the absence may be an indication of good cause but is not
conclusive. Taking days off work for no apparent reason is not good cause. Examples of good cause
include where the absence is due to


1. bereavement or sudden serious illness in the family or


2. a disaster at home or


3. suspension from work, whether or not on full pay or


4. a requirement to attend court.
