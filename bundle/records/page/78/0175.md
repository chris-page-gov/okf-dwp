Example 3 - Additional amount for carers

Sanjay, who is aged 65, is a member of a polygamous marriage and is married to Gita and Bina. Sanjay is
entitled to RP of £127.25 a week and Bina gets RP of £79.60 a week. Both Gita and Bina are entitled to
CA and each satisfy the conditions for the award of an additional amount for carers.
