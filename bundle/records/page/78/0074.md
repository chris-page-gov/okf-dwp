Example


Katja lives in a self-contained granny flat attached to her daughter’s house. She has her own bathroom,
kitchen, bedroom and living room. Access to the flat is from the hall of her daughter’s house and the
front door of the property is shared. Everything else is separate.


The hall is the only shared area and is a communal area. Katja does not share the accommodation and
does not normally reside with her daughter.
