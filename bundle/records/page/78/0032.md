Qualifying conditions

78126 A claimant is entitled to a transitional amount if 1


1. the claimant was entitled to IS, JSA(IB) or ESA(IR) in respect of the day before the relevant day and


2. on the relevant day, the relevant amount exceeds the provisional amount.


                                                                 1 SPC Regs, reg 6(6)(b); Sch I, para 6(1) & (2)



The relevant day

78127 The relevant day is the day in respect of which the claimant is first entitled to SPC 1. That is their
first day of entitlement to SPC.


                                                                                  1 SPC Regs, Sch I, para 6(3)



The relevant amount

78128 The relevant amount1 is the amount which, on the day before the relevant day, was the claimant’s
applicable amount for IS, JSA(IB) or ESA(IR), less any


1. personal allowances for dependants (see DMG 23039) 2


2. Res A3


3. FP (see DMG 23076)4


4. DCP (see DMG 23242)5


5. EDP for dependants (see DMG 23181)6.


Note: See DMG 78129 if a transitional addition was in payment on the IS/JSA(IB) claim.
