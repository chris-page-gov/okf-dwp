Deductions from eligible housing costs

78465 Deductions should be made from eligible housing costs1 where they include an amount for


1. fuel charges or


2. ineligible service charges or


3. repairs and improvements.


                                                                              1 SPC Regs, Sch II, para 13(2)


Deductions for fuel charges
78466 Where any of the charges for eligible housing costs in DMG 78455 include an amount for fuel

charges, the following deductions should be made1


1. a standard fuel deduction for


      1.1 heating (other than hot water) £35.25


      1.2 hot water £4.10


      1.3 lighting £2.85


      1.4 cooking £4.10 or


2. if the claimant provides evidence that the


      2.1 actual amount or


      2.2 estimated amount


      for fuel is different to the standard deductions in 1., the deduction should be varied to the amounts
      in 2.


                                           1 SPC Regs, Sch II, para 13(2)(a); HB (Gen) Regs, Sch 1, para 6(2)
