Disabled person in hospital
78857 The disabled person that Bob looks after is in hospital and their entitlement to AA is lost after
four weeks. Bob is treated as satisfying the conditions for the additional amount for carers for a further
eight weeks. After twelve weeks in hospital, the additional amount for carers is withdrawn. Bob is then
entitled to SC only, calculated as follows:
