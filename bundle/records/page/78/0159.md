Permanent or indefinite contract
60 A recognizable cycle of work can exist at the outset of employment. This would happen where a
person has a permanent or indefinite contract that expressly provides for a cycle. The contract may
expressly provide for periods of work and periods of no work, for example, school holidays for school
ancillary workers are usually periods of no work.


Example


Julia works as a school clerk under an indefinite contract that provides for work during school terms and
no work during school holidays. The DM decides that Julia’s contract establishes a cycle from its outset.
