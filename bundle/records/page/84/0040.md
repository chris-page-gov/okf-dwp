Payment made after Carer's Allowance reassessment following Sayce Review

84365 A payment received on or after 16.7.26 following a recalculation of CA under the Sayce Review 1

is disregarded indefinitely. Such a payment may be received where2


1. a decision was made about the claimant’s CA entitlement due to fluctuating earnings between 10.4.15
and 2.9.25 and


2. that decision led to an overpayment of CA and


3. following a reassessment conducted since 13.4.26 the Secretary of State determines that the
claimant’s earnings should have been averaged.


Note: Arrears of CA that are not due to the reassessment exercise in 3. are not disregarded.


                 1 Independent Review of Carer’s Allowance Overpayments; 2 SPC Regs, Sch V, para 20AB


Example 1


Freyja was in receipt of CA as a carer for her sister Greta but had also taken irregular shift work at a local
convenience store, so that her earnings fluctuated and occasionally exceeded the earnings limit for CA
four times in a nine-week period. On 10.12.24 a decision was made that Freyja had been overpaid
£327.60 and that amount was recoverable as Freyja had not informed the Department of these earnings.
However, after a review exercise concluded on 17.7.26 it was determined that, if the earnings had been
averaged over the nine-week period, Freyja’s earnings would have been under the earnings limit. The
overpayment is cancelled and Freyja is refunded £327.60, representing a restoration of her CA
entitlement. The £327.60 is disregarded indefinitely from Freyja’s total capital when determining her
SPC entitlement.


Example 2


Anton receives arrears of CA of £4,853.45 following a successful appeal to the FtT after his claim made
on 13.4.25 was refused. As these arrears are not associated with the Sayce Review they are included in
full in Anton’s capital from the date of receipt.
