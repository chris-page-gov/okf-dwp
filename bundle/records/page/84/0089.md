Arrears and concessionary payments of £5000 or more

When the disregard applies
[See Memo DMG 15/20]


84671 This disregard applies where


1. a person receives arrears of one of the


      1.1 benefits or


      1.2 allowances or


      1.3 payments


listed in DMG 84567 and, if appropriate, any concessionary payment to compensate for the late
payment of that benefit and


2. the total of the arrears and any concessionary payment is £5000 or more and


3. the total of arrears and any concessionary payment is paid to rectify or compensate for an


      3.1 official error (see DMG 03256) or


      3.2 error of law (see DMG 04280) and


4. the total of arrears and any concessionary payment is received in full by the person


      4.1 on or after the day on which entitlement to SPC began 1 or


      4.2 in respect of a payment disregarded for the purposes of


            4.2.a IS


            4.2.b JSA


            4.2.c HB
