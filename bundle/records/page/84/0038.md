Annuities



84357 An annuity is


1. a fixed sum payable at specified intervals in return for a premium paid either


      1.1 by instalments or


      1.2 in a single payment and


2. normally taken out to provide an income in retirement.


The value of the right to receive income from and the surrender value of an annuity are disregarded

indefinitely1.


Note: This disregard applies only for the purposes of determining deemed weekly income.
