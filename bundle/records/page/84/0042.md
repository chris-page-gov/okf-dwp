Meaning of “reasonable period”
84376 When determining what represents a reasonable period for the sale or disposal of an asset, the
DM should have regard to


1. the date that the duty to dispose of the asset arose


2. the nature of the asset and the period within which that particular type of asset would normally be
expected to be sold or disposed


3. any legal obligations and restrictions existing that could affect both the sale and disposal of any
assets. For example, in a partnership situation, the provisions of the partnership deed and the
Partnership Act 1890 could place restrictions and subsequent delays on both the sale of assets, and the
disposal of any cash assets resulting from the sale.


Meaning of self-employed earner
84377 S/E earner means a person


1. who is in gainful employment in GB (see DMG 070702) and


2. whose employment is not the same as that of an employed earner.


A S/E earner includes people employed outside of GB in employment which would be classed as
employment as a S/E earner if the employment was in GB. It also includes people undertaking

employment as a S/E earner and that employment is disregarded when working out SS contributions 1.
