About the guidance

84121 This part gives guidance on how to work out if a person is the beneficial owner of capital in certain
types of cases.


84122 The guidance in this part involves principles of law. The law in England and Wales can be different
from the law in Scotland but the outcome may be the same. The guidance will say if the outcome is
different.
