Note: For the purposes of DMG 84701 6.1 a person can fall within the term “excluded occupier” if they
share the living space of the property with the claimant. However, this does not give the person any
rights against eviction. For a person to acquire rights against eviction the nature of any licence to remain
should be such that it can be determined by giving reasonable notice.

84702 There are additional factors if the capital is jointly held and real or heritable property (see DMG

        1.1 are willing and able to buy the share


        1.2 will agree to the sale of the asset as a whole


        1.3 are occupying the property and, if so, whether they would be willing to vacate the property


2. whether the courts would order


        2.1 the sale of the property as a whole or


        2.2 the partition of the property


        if the other owners will not buy the share or agree to the sale of the asset as a whole


3. the length of time the buyer of the share may have to wait to get possession if the matter goes to
