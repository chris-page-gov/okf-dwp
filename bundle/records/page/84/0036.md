All other capital that is disregarded is disregarded for the purposes of calculating income.


 1 SPC Regs, Sch V, Part II, para 24; 2 Sch V, Part II, para 25; 3 Sch V, Part II, para 26; 4 Sch V, Part II, para
                                                                                                               28


Onus of proof
84354 The person who has the capital has to show the capital can be disregarded. Capital is included
when working out the amount of deemed weekly income if there is no evidence to show it can be
disregarded.


What the DM decides
84355 The DM decides if capital can be disregarded. This is done


1. before the capital is valued as the DM does not have to know the value of the capital to decide if it can
be disregarded and


2. before working out the amount of deemed weekly income from capital.


The DM also decides how long the capital is disregarded and, for certain disregards, whether it is
reasonable to disregard the capital for a longer period.


Period of disregard
84356 Capital which can be disregarded is disregarded for1


1. an indefinite period (DMG 84367 et seq) or


2. up to two years (DMG 84521 et seq) or


3. a year or longer (DMG 84561 et seq) or


4. 26 weeks or longer (DMG 84601 et seq) or


5. the remainder of an award of SPC (DMG 84671 et seq).


                                                                                              1 SPC Regs, Sch V
