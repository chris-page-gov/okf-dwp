# DMG Vol 14 Ch 83: Assessed income periods — PDF page 13

```text
        Example

        Henry aged 66 makes a claim for SPC on 10.3.09. He asks that his claim be
        considered from 11.12.08.

        The DM decides to backdate the claim by three months to 11.12.08.

        On 17.3.09 the DM decides to award SPC to Henry and that it is payable from
        15.12.08 (the first payday following 11.12.08).

        The DM decides that he can set a five year AIP. He decides that the AIP starts on
        11.12.08 and ends on 10.12.13.

        Note: New claims to SPC with a TAM date on, or after 6.4.16 will have a 3 year PCR
        set.

        83048 - 83049




Vol 14 Amendment 40 June 2016
```
