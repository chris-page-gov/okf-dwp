Treatment of income for the purpose of making part-week payments

79104 Income is taken into account for the purposes of calculating part-week payments of the GC 1 (see
DMG 79105).


                                                                       1 SPC Act 02, s 2; SPC Regs, reg 13A



Amount of part-week payments

79105 Where a part-week payment is appropriate, the DM should1


1. calculate the weekly rate of the GC taking into account any income received for that week and


2. divide the amount calculated at 1. by 7 and


3. multiply the result by the number of days in the part-week and


4. round any fraction of a penny


      4.1 up if its half a penny or more or


      4.2 down if its less than half a penny2.


The resulting amount is the amount of GC which is payable in respect of the part-week from the end of
the IS, ESA(IR) or JSA(IB) award up to and including the day before the first SPC pay day.
