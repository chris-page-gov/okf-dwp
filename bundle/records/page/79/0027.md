Meaning of hostel 79500-79503


Rules for third party deductions 79504-79549




Meaning of hostel

79500 A hostel is1 a building other than a care home, independent hospital or Abbeyfield Home which


1. is


        1.1 managed or owned by a housing association registered with the Regulator of Social Housing or

        the Welsh Ministers2 or


        1.2 managed or owned by a housing association registered with the Scottish Ministers 3 or


        1.3 operated other than on a commercial basis and funded wholly or partly by a government
        department or an LA or


        1.4 managed by a voluntary organization or charity and provides care, support or supervision for
        the purpose of


              1.4.a rehabilitation or


              1.4.b resettlement within the community and


2. provides accommodation not in separate and self-contained premises and facilities adequate for the
needs of those living in the hostel including


        2.1 board or


        2.2 water or


        2.3 a service charge for fuel or


        2.4 meals or


        2.5 laundry or


        2.6 cleaning (other than communal areas).
