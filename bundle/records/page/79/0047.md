Meaning of water charges

79660 For England and Wales water charges means any water or sewerage charges under prescribed

legislation1. Where these are charged separately or by different water authorities see DMG 79687.


Note: The definitions of water charges are different for the purposes of third party deductions from
benefit (see DMG 79700 for guidance on water charges for Scotland).


                              1 SS (C&P) Regs, Sch 9, para 1(1), Water Industry Act 1991, Part 5, Chapter 1
