Meaning of water charges

79700 For Scotland, water charges means any water or sewerage charges established by Scottish

Water under a charges scheme made under prescribed legislation 1. Water charges can be made


1. with rent or


2. by a meter or


3. unmetered.


                              1 SS (C&P) Regs, Sch 9, para 1(1), Water Industry (Scotland) Act 2002, s 29A


79701 In Scotland, LAs have responsibility for the collection and billing of all water charges. Sewerage
charges are included in the CT charge and water charges are collected through the council water charge.


79702 Where there is a mixture of water and CT arrears or only CT arrears, the LA treats the arrears for
water and CT as a single debt and may request a single third party deduction to cover both items (see
DMG 79705).




Rules for third party deductions

79703 When water charges are paid with, or as part of, rent, third party deductions may be made on the

same basis as in England and Wales1 (see DMG 79550 et seq).
