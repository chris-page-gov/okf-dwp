Payments of SPC for less than a full week in other circumstances

79106 Payments of SPC may be made for a period of less than a full week where 1


1. a claimant in receipt of SPC is awarded another social security benefit and


2. because of the award of the other social security benefit the claimant’s benefit week changes or is
expected to change.


                                                                     1 SS CS (D&A) Regs, Sch 3B, para 4(b)


79107 Where a payment of SPC is appropriate for a period of less than a full week and the claimant has
an income to be taken into account in that period, the DM should take that income into account in the
normal way (see DMG Chapters 85 and 86).
