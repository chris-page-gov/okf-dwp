Maximum savings credit from 6.04.20
46 The MSC is a fixed percentage of the difference between the SMG and the SCT (see DMG 77363).
From 6.04.20, that percentage is 60%.


So from 6.04.20 the amount of the MSC is as follows


1. claimant with no partner £173.75 SMG - £150.47 SCT x 60% = £13.97


2. claimant with a partner £265.20 SMG - £239.17 SCT x 60% = £15.62


3. polygamous marriages £265.20 SMG - £239.17 SCT x 60% = £15.62


4. prisoners Nil


5. members of religious orders fully maintained by their order (see DMG 78680) Nil


6. people in hospital - see DMG 78700.
