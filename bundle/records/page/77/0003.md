Introduction 77001 - 77003

Scope of this Chapter

77001 SPC is a SS benefit1 that was introduced on 6.10.032. It is made up of


1. a GC, to ensure a minimum level of income for people who have reached the qualifying age (see DMG
77330) and


2. a SC which, from age 65, provides an additional amount for pensioners who have low or modest
savings and income (see DMG 77350167).


Note 1: See DMG 77031 for guidance on qualifying age.


Note 2: From 6.12.183 a person must be at least pensionable age4 (see DMG Chapter 75) to be entitled to
a SC.


                   1 SPC Act 02, s 1(1); 2 SPC Regs, reg 1(1); 3 Pensions Act 07, s 13(3); 4 SPC Act 02, s 3(1)


77002 This Chapter contains basic guidance on SPC and explains


1. the conditions of entitlement for SPC


2. how to establish membership of the same household


3. how to calculate the normal amount of SPC payable.


77003 For guidance on


1. the additional amounts of GC that may be awarded - see DMG Chapter 78


2. the special rules that apply when calculating how much SPC to pay


        2.1 to members of polygamous marriages - see DMG Chapter 78


        2.2 to prisoners - see DMG Chapter 78


        2.3 to members of religious orders - see DMG Chapter 78


        2.4 when people are in hospital - see DMG Chapter 78


        2.5 when people are subject to the loss of benefit provisions - see ADM Chapter B2


        2.6 when people are absent from GB - see DMG Chapters 07 and 78


3. payment questions - see DMG Chapter 79
