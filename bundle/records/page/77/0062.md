56 The MSC is a fixed percentage of the difference between the SMG and the SCT (see DMG 77363).

So from 11.04.22 the amount of the MSC is as follows


1. claimant with no partner £182.60 SMG - £158.47 SCT x 60% = £14.48


2. claimant with a partner £278.70 SMG - £251.70 SCT x 60% = £16.20


3. polygamous marriages £278.70 SMG - £251.70 SCT x 60% = £16.20
