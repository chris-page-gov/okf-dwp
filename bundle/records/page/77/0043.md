Rounding of fractions
77376 Where the calculation of Amount A or Amount B results in a fraction of a penny, it should be

rounded in the claimant’s favour1. So when calculating


1. Amount A (as in DMG 77371), any fraction should be rounded up to the next whole penny, if in the
claimant’s favour and


2. Amount B (as in DMG 77373), any fraction should be rounded down to the next whole penny, if in the
claimant’s favour.


                                                                                    1 SPC Regs, reg 7(4);
