Example 12: Couple - Mixed income types in payment

Ina, who is 70, has RP of £79.60 and a personal pension of £40. Her partner Jamie gets IB of £55.90 and
WDisP of £30. Jamie’s IB is not QI and £10 of his WDisP is disregarded in the calculation of income (see
DMG Chapter 85). Ina qualifies for an additional amount for housing costs (see DMG Chapter 78) of £30.
