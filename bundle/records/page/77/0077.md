Example 7: Single - Income less than AMG

Carlos, who is aged 70, is severely disabled and lives alone. He is entitled to basic RP of £79.60 and has
£3 a week assumed income from capital (see DMG Chapter 84). Carlos qualifies for an additional amount
of £44.15 a week because he is severely disabled (see DMG 78040 et seq).


Calculation of GC:

 Additional amounts                          £44.15


 AMG                                         £149.60


 Less income (£79.60 + £3)                   £82.60


 Amount of GC                                £67.00


Calculation of SC:


 SCT                                         £79.60


 QI                                          £82.60


 QI exceeds SCT by                           £3


 60% x £3                                    £1.80


 MSC                                         £15.51


 Amount A                                    £1.80


 Amount B (income does not exceed            Nil
 AMG)


 Amount of SC                                £1.80


Total SPC payable                            £68.80


Calculation of total weekly income:


 RP                                          £79.60


 Assumed income from capital                 £3
