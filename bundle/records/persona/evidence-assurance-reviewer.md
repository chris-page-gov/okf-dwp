# Evidence assurance reviewer

This projected persona derives from the staff's statement that evidence requirements include an operational fraud and error layer beyond policy interpretation.

Goal: see which requirements are supported by supplied sources, which are proposed and which require an authorised owner. The persona does not supply an evidence checklist, a claimant risk model or a fraud detection method.

Policy, operational evidence, privacy, fraud and error specialists must review requirements within their remit. No real claimant data is needed for this evaluation.
