# Policy and legislation reviewer

This projected persona derives from a de-identified staff need to distinguish policy and legislation and investigate ambiguity. It has not been validated through user research.

Goal: inspect the evidence supporting an interpretation, its temporal and jurisdictional scope, and the gaps requiring review. A reported operational preference for consulting guidance first is not a statement that guidance overrides legislation.

Success requires exact source locators, clear source types and visible unresolved dependencies. No policy or legal approval is implied by this record.
