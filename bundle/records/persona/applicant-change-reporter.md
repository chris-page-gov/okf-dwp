# Applicant or change reporter

This projected persona responds to the owner's request for application and change-of-circumstances journeys. It has not been validated through research with applicants.

Goal: understand a plain-English next step, recognise missing information and reach the appropriate official service or human help. An initial application and a report of a change are separate scenario variants with separate triggers and completion criteria.

All evaluation cases are synthetic. This record does not establish official service steps, collect personal data or determine entitlement.
