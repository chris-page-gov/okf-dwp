Chapter 80 is retained in the source inventory and the scope note because it contains transitional provisions. It is not merged into the default seven-chapter search corpus. Chapters 81 and 82 are explicitly spare.

## Follow an evidence route

1. Open the relevant chapter and source page.
2. Keep definitions, exceptions, dates, examples and footnotes together.
3. Follow any referenced memo, legislation or other chapter on the official source.
4. Record which dependencies remain unverified before making a domain claim.

The graph edges from this research aid to chapters are authored navigational references. They are not formal legal dependencies or a transcription of every cross-reference in the corpus.
