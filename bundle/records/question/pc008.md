# Can this bundle tell me what I am entitled to?

Independent project-authored statement of scope.

No. This exemplar supports source discovery and inspection. It does not evaluate individual facts, provide current legal consolidation, calculate an award or replace qualified advice. No personal claimant data is needed for the demonstration.
