Introduction
1. The purpose of this memo is to give guidance to decision makers (DM) on The Social Security (Scotland) Act
2018 (Disability Assistance) (Consequential Amendments) Order 2024. The amendments came into force on

21.10.24.1


                                                                         1 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024




Background

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect the introduction by the Scottish Government
(SG) of the Pension Age Disability Payment (PADP). This is to replace Attendance Allowance (AA) for pension age
adults resident in Scotland.


                                                                         1 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024
Income Support and Jobseeker's Allowance

Definitions

3. DMG 23071 defines Attendance allowance for JSA(IB) and IS purposes 1. From 21.10.2024 PADP2 is to be
included in these definitions.

Regular Carers

4. Entitlement for individuals regularly and substantially engaged in caring for another person 1 will now extend to

those caring for someone who is in receipt of PADP 2 (DMG 20116).


             1 IS (Gen) Regs, Sch IB, para 4(a); SS CB Act 92, s 70(2) 2 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024


Remunerative work exclusion

5. From 21.10.2024, individuals regularly and substantially engaged in caring 1 for someone receiving PADP2 will

be treated as not being in remunerative work2 (DMG 20502 and 20252).

Claims for linked benefits
8. For the purposes of the date of claim rules, PADP is a ‘qualifying benefit’ (DMG 02264) (DMG 02263) if an award

of PADP makes a relevant benefit either payable or payable at an increased rate 1. This means the rules that, in
certain circumstances, allow a claim for a relevant benefit to be backdated to the date from which another benefit

has been awarded or made payable (DMG 02267-8, 02269-70, 02273 and 02277)2 will apply where the other
benefit is PADP. Also, the condition in DMG 02270.4 is satisfied where a further claim for a relevant benefit is made
within 3 months of the date on which it is decided that PADP is payable again as a result of the claimant leaving a
care home or hospital3.

Revision and Supersession

Definitions of 'Scottish disability benefit'
9. The definition of ‘Scottish disability benefit’ for revision and supersession purposes 1 has been amended so that it
now includes PADP. The consequences of this change are explained below.

Supersession of a SPC award because of entitlement to PADP
10. Where


       1. an award of SPC is superseded on the ground that there has been a relevant change of circumstances
       since the award took effect and


       2. the change of circumstances is that the claimant or partner has become entitled to


              2. 1. the ADP daily living component of adult disability payment at the standard or enhanced rate or


              2. 2. the PADP pension age disability payment


      the superseding decision takes effect from the beginning of the benefit week in which the first day of the new

       ADP or PADP entitlement falls or, if that is not practicable, on the first day of the following benefit week 1.

Additional amounts for the Severely disabled

Special Groups
14. DMG 78034 et seq provides guidance on the qualifying conditions for, and rates of, the extra amount for

severe disability for single claimants and claimants with partners 1. (For polygamous marriages2, see DMG 78611 –
78614.) From 21.10.24 PADP is added to the list of qualifying benefits when considering entitlement.


                                                          1 SPC Regs, Sch I, para 1(1) 2 SPC Regs, Sch III, para 1(9)




People whose presence is ignored
15. DMG 78077 explains that DMs can ignore the presence of persons in receipt of certain benefits when

determining a claimant’s entitlement to the extra amount for severe disability 1. From 21.10.24 PADP is added to the
list of benefits.


                                                                                       1 SPC Regs, Sch I, para 2(2)(a)


Deductions for non-dependants
16. Where a claimant or partner is in receipt of certain benefits, no non-dependant deduction is applied 1. From
21.10.24, PADP is added to the list of those benefits. PADP is included in the list of benefits to be disregarded

when calculating the gross weekly income of a non-dependant 2. (DMG 78524).


                                                          1 SPC Regs, Sch II, para 14(6)(b)(ia); 2 Sch II, para 14(8)(a)
Amounts disregarded from earnings
17. Where a claimant or partner is in receipt of certain benefits, £20 a week is disregarded from the earnings of the

claimant or partner1. From 21.10.24, PADP is added to the list of benefits which qualify for the £20 disregard.


                                                                                  1 SPC Regs, Sch VI, para 4(1)(a)(iiia)



Social Fund Funeral Payments

Deductions to an award
18. SFMFE DMG 39408 and Appendix 1 to DMG Chapter 39 gives guidance on arrears of benefit payable to the
deceased not being counted as assets of the deceased when considering deductions from the amount of award of
funeral payment. From 21.10.24 arrears of PADP payable to the deceased as at the date of death are added to the

list of benefits excluded from the assets of the deceased 1.


                                                                                   1 SFMFE (Gen) Regs, reg 10(1A)(gg)




Employment and Support Allowance

Caring for another person

19. Claimants’ partners are treated as not being in remunerative work where they are regularly and substantially

engaged in caring for another person1 that is in receipt of a relevant benefit. From 21.10.24 PADP 2 has been
added to the list of relevant benefits. (DMG 41514)


            1 ESA Regs, reg 43(2)(c); 2 IS (Gen) Regs, Sch IB, para 4; 2 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024




Carer's Allowance

Meaning of 'Severely disabled person'

20. For the purposes of entitlement to CA, DMG 60033 et seq defines a severely disabled person 1 as someone
eligible for certain specified benefits. Effective from 21.10.24, PADP will be included in this list of qualifying
benefits.
