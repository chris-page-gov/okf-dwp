introduction


    1. This memo gives guidance on the Social Security (Income and Capital Disregards) (Amendment)

       Regulations 20231 [SS (I&C Dis) (Amdt) Regs 2023], SI 2023/640. The changes take effect from
       9.7.23.


                                                         1 SS (I&C Dis) (Amdt) Regs 2023; SI 2023/640


Background


    2. These regulations1 ensure that certain payments are disregarded for the purposes of calculating
       entitlement to IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR) and SPC.
                                                                        1 SS (I&C Dis) (Amdt) Regs 2023




    3. These regulations1


   1. expand the existing disregard for Grenfell Tower payments
   2. create a new disregard for Post Office compensation payments, and

   3. ensure that partners of persons in respect of whom a payment under the relevant legislation 2 is
      made are covered by a disregard.


                                1 SS (I&C Dis) (Amdt) Regs 2023; 2 Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979


    4. These amendment regulations1 also provide that


   1. Grenfell Tower payments and
   2. Post Office compensation payments


will be exempt from the Department’s compensation recovery scheme2. This means that compensation
payments will not be reduced.


Note: Payments made under relevant legislation3 to or in respect of the injured person are already
exempt.

Qualifying Person


    9. In the definition of qualifying person, a


   1. Post Office compensation payment or
   2. Vaccine Damage payment


is inserted into relevant legislation1. This means prescribed persons that receive a payment from these
schemes is added to the definition of a qualifying person.

Vaccine Damage Payments


15. In relation to vaccine damage payments the disregard at paragraph 9 applies where


     1. a person who is severely disabled as a result of vaccination receives a payment under relevant
        legislation or
     2. the partner of a person referred to in 1. receives a payment which is derived from
            1. that payment from that person or
            2. their estate or
     3. the payment is made to the personal representative of a person who was
            1. severely disabled as a result of vaccination or
            2. to their partner from their estate.


                                                                      1 Vaccine Damage Payments Act 1979




NOTIONAL CAPITAL


16. For IS1, JSA(IB)2 & ESA(IR)3, any Post Office compensation payment or vaccine damage payment paid
to


     1. a third party for the claimant or their partner or
     2. the claimant or their partner for a third party


is not to be treated as notional capital.

17. Fully disregard1


   1. any Grenfell Tower payments or Post Office compensation payments in IS, JSA(IB) and ESA(IR)
   and


   2. any income from capital that was a Grenfell Tower payment or Post Office compensation
   payment in IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR) and SPC.


Note: Grenfell Tower payments and Post Office compensation payments are not prescribed income for
SPC.
