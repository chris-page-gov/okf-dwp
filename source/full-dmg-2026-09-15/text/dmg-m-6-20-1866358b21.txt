INTRODUCTION

1. This memo gives guidance on the Social Security (Coronavirus) (Further
     Measures) Regulations1 which came into force on 30.3.20.

     Note: The regulations cease to have effect on 31.8.212.

                                 1 SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs SI 2020 No. 371; 2 reg 10(2B)


2. The regulations

1.      dis-apply the application of periods of sickness in JSA for those affected by
        Coronavirus disease – see paragraphs 6 – 7 and

2.      enable CA claimants who are temporarily unable to care for the severely
        disabled person where either of them is affected by Coronavirus disease to
        retain entitlement to CA – see paragraph 8.

3. A person is affected by Coronavirus disease (also known as COVID-19)1 if they are

1.      infected or contaminated with Coronavirus disease or

2.      in isolation (see paragraphs 4-5) or
3.      caring for a child or qualifying young person2 who is a member of the person’s
        household and who is

        3.1     in isolation or

        3.2     infected or contaminated with Coronavirus disease.

     Note: For the purposes of 3., a child or qualifying young person is a member of the
     person’s household if they live with the person caring for them.

                         1 SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs, reg 1(2) & 8(2); 2 SS C&B Act 92, s. 142


Meaning of “isolation”

4. A person is in isolation if they are separate from any other person so as to prevent
     infection or contamination with Coronavirus disease1. DMs should have regard to
     the latest UK Government advice to the public on when they should self-isolate on
     a case by case basis.

     Note: A person can be in isolation when they live with other people such as family
     members or in multiple occupancy dwellings.

                                                        1 SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs, reg 1(2)


5. This does not include where a person

1.      has not been advised to isolate or

2.      is looking after a child or qualifying young person whose school or nursery has
        been closed or

3.      is following UK Government advice on social distancing.



JSA: PERIODS OF SICKNESS

6. Where a JSA claimant

1.      is affected by Coronavirus disease as in paragraph 3 and

2.      satisfies the JSA conditions of entitlement apart from the condition that they do
        not have LCW 1

     they are treated as not having LCW 2.
     Note: See DMG Chapter 20 for guidance on short and extended periods of
     sickness.

                                      1 JS Act 95 s1(2); 2 SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs, reg 8(1)


7. Any period where paragraph 6 applies is disregarded for the purposes of calculating
     short periods and extended period of sickness1.

                         1 JSA Regs, reg 55(3) & 55ZA(5); SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs, reg 8(2)




CA: TREATED AS CARING

8. DMG 60045 – 60049 gives guidance on the effect of temporary breaks in caring for
     the severely disabled person1. From 30.3.20 where the CA claimant is temporarily
     unable to care for the severely disabled person because the

1.      CA claimant or

2.      severely disabled person for whom CA is paid

     is affected by Coronavirus disease as in paragraph 3, the conditions in DMG 60045
     2. and 3. are modified so that they do not apply2. This means that the CA claimant
     can continue to be treated as caring for the severely disabled person, and remain
     entitled to CA, for more than the four or twelve weeks periods in DMG 60046.

                                 1 SS (ICA) Regs, reg 4(2); 2 SS (Coronavirus) (Further Measures) Regs, reg 9
