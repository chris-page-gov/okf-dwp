         Introduction
         General

078060 This part of the chapter describes the special rules that apply to

         1.     mariners

         2.     airmen

         3.     continental shelf workers and

         4.     members of Her Majesty's Forces.

         Mariner

078070   For Industrial Injuries benefits a mariner is a person employed under a contract of
         service

         1.    on board any ship or vessel as a master or as a crew member or as a pilot on
               board or in any other capacity on board and
                                                                  1
         2.    treated as employed earner's employment .
                                                                           1 SS (II) (Mariners’ Ben) Regs, reg 1(2)


078071   For other benefits a mariner is a person employed under a contract of service on
         board any ship or vessel

         1.    as a master or

         2.    as a crew member or

         3.    in any other capacity on board when the employment in that other capacity is
               with the ship or vessel or the crew or any passengers or cargo or mail carried
               and the contract is entered into in the UK with a view to its performance (in
               whole or in part) while the ship or vessel is on its voyage.

078072   This does not include a serving member of the forces except those undergoing
                                                                                                        1
         training or instruction in certain prescribed establishments and organizations for 72
                                      2
         consecutive hours or less .
                                          1 SS (II) (Mariners’ Ben) Regs, reg 1(2); 2 SS (Conts) Regs, Sch 6, Part 1


078073   The following expressions used in the Merchant Shipping Acts have the same
                                  1
         meaning as in the Acts

         “Chief Officer of Customs” includes the

         •     collector

         •     superintendent

         •     principal coast officer or

         •     other chief officer of Customs at each post.

         “Superintendent”, so far as respects a British possession, includes any

         •     Shipping master or

         •     Other officer carrying out the duties of a superintendent.

         “Consular Officer” when used in connection with a foreign country, means the officer
         recognized by Her Majesty as a consular officer of that foreign country.
                                                                              1 Merchant Shipping Acts 1894-1981

         Continental shelf worker

078080   A continental shelf worker is a person who is or has been in prescribed employment
         on the continental shelf.

         Definition of prescribed employment
                                                                  1
078081 Prescribed employment means any employment , whether under a contract of
                                                                      2
         service or not, in any appointed or designated area , but see DMG Volume 1 ­
         Decision Making and Appeals.
                                        1 Oil and Gas (Enterprise) Act 1982, s 23(2); 2 SS Ben (PA) Regs, reg 11(1)


078082 Designated area means any area set out by Order in Council under the Continental
         Shelf Act 1964 as an area where the UK may explore the seabed, subsoil and
                                            1
         exploit their natural resources .
                                                                                     1 SS Ben (PA) Regs, reg 11(1)


078083   Prescribed area means any area over which Norway or any EC country (except the
         UK) exercises sovereign rights to explore the seabed and subsoil and exploit the
         natural resources or other named areas (but see DMG 078081). This area must be
         outside the territorial water of

         1.     Norway or

         2.     any EC country (except the UK).

          Place for return

                                                                                                              1
078123    The employer is responsible for returning a mariner to the "place for return" . There
          is no provision for any person or authority to decide the correct place of return. If the
          question arises, the decision maker should decide whether any "place" is the correct
                                 2
          "place for return" where the mariner is returned to depends upon where that person
          resides in Great Britain. However, a mariner can agree a place of return with the
          employer.
               1 Merchant Shipping (Repatriation) Regs, reg 3(1); 2 R(S) 4/64; Merchant Shipping (Repatriation) Regs, reg 7


078124    Where a mariner is returned to depends on whether that person resides in Great
          Britain. However, a mariner can agree with the employer to be returned anywhere.



          Resident in United Kingdom

078125    The place for return for a mariner who is resident in the United Kingdom is the
          closest of the following to the place of return to the United Kingdom

          1.       the mariner's home in the United Kingdom or

          2.       the place where the Merchant Navy Establishment Administration Office at
                   where the mariner is registered is situated.

          Not resident in the United Kingdom

078126    The place for return for a mariner who is not resident in the United Kingdom is the

078127    The mariner should be returned to any other place agreed to by the mariner and the
