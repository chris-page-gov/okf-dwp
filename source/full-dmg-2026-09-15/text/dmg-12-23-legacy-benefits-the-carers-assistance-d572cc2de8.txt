Introduction

1. [See DMG memo 01-26] This memo gives guidance on The Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support

Payment) (Scotland) Regulations 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Order 2023 1 which come into
force on 19.11.23.


                                                                                        1 SI 2023 No 1214



Background

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect the introduction of the Carer
Support Payment (CSP) which replaces CA in Scotland.


                                                                   1 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23(CA) Order 23




Carer’s Allowance

3. From 19.11.23 a claimant cannot have entitlement to both CA and CSP for the same day 1. Additionally,
where two or more people are caring for the same severely disabled person, only one of them will be
entitled to either CA or CSP for any day. It will be up to them to choose and elect who will be entitled to

CA or CSP2 by giving the DM a notice in writing signed by both carers specifying who should be entitled

to CA or CSP3. If there is no election the Secretary of State should be satisfied that one carer has the

relevant entitlement to the benefit, following consultation with the Scottish DM 4.
Note: When a DM encounters a rival carer situation, a structured consultation process with the Scottish
DM’s, carefully considering all the key factors, should take place to ensure that the most appropriate
carer is chosen.


                                                  1 SS CB Act 92, s 70(7); 2 s 70(7ZA), s70(7ZB), s70(7ZC);
                        3 SS (ICA) Regs, reg 7(1) & 7(1A); 4 SS CB Act 92, s 70(7ZA), s70(7ZB) & s70(7ZC).


Example 1
Mary and Sharon both care for Henry for at least 35 hours a week each. Mary lives in England and claims
CA. Sharon lives in Scotland and claims CSP. Only one of them will be entitled to benefit for caring for
Henry. Mary and Sharon decide themselves that Mary should have entitlement. Therefore, Mary receives
CA. Sharon is not entitled to CSP for the period Mary is entitled to CA.


Example 2
David and Eric both care for Susan for at least 35 hours a week each. David lives in England and claims
CA. Eric lives in Scotland and claims CSP. Only one of them will be entitled to benefit for caring for Susan.
David and Eric both believe they should be entitled to claim and cannot decide between them who should
be entitled. The DM should consult with the Scottish DM to determine which carer has the relevant
entitlement.

Lump Sum Payments
4. DMG 75405 et seq provides guidance on people entitled to a lump sum payment when they defer
their RP. The amount of RP which would have been payable had it not been deferred does not include

any pension, where for all of an accrual period, a person has been in receipt of CSP 1.

Income support

Prescribed category of person
7. People are entitled to IS if they meet the conditions of entitlement, including where they are in a

prescribed category1 (see DMG 20081 et seq). This includes people who care for another person and are

entitled to CA2. From 19.11.23, the category includes people who care for another person and are entitled

to CSP3.

reference to a CSP1.

Definition

any amount of CSP that is in excess of the amount the claimant would receive if they had an entitlement

to CA2.

Notional income
24. DMG 85447 explains that where a claimant to whom DMG 85441 applies is in receipt of an
overlapping benefit or allowance the amount of RP income they shall be treated as having is the RP they

are entitled to minus the amount of the benefit or allowance in payment 1. From 19.11.23 CSP is included

as such an overlapping benefit or allowance2.

34. DMG 75549 et seq and 63062 et seq provides guidance that a person who is engaged in caring can

receive a class 3 credit towards satisfying the single contribution condition. This will include a person
engaged in caring for a period of up to 12 weeks,


1. before they become entitled to CSP1 or


2. after they cease to be entitled to CSP2


However, 2. does not apply if a claimant continues to be entitled to a credit after entitlement to CSP
ends3.

Additional Pension and Applications for NI credits
35. See DMG 75556 and 63069 for guidance on applications for NI credits when engaged in caring. NI
credits cannot be awarded in respect of claims to CSP, or the Carer Credit, for any period where the
person was a married woman who had elected to pay a reduced rate of NI contributions (Married

Woman's Reduced Rate Election)1.
