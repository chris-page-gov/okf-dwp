                  Decision Makers Guide
Amended Chapters for February 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 8: Employment and Support Allowance

Chapter 41: ESA conditions of entitlement
Incorporate Memo DMG 25/16 about changes to collection of Class 2 NI
contributions.

Chapter 42: Limited capability for work and limited capability for
work-related activity
Incorporates Memo DMG 13/17 (ESA: further evidence of LCW). This memo was
about changes to the guidance for HCPs (Health care professionals) about when to
obtain further evidence. That guidance has now been incorporated into the HCP
guidance. Note added at paragraph 42160 to refer DMs to existing guidance on the
role of medical services in gathering information for the WCA.

Chapter 44: Normal amount payable and components
All references to ‘CE’ changed to say carer element (to avoid any confusion with
child element); Pensioner Premium updated at paragraph 44094 et seq to reflect the
changes to the categories of persons excluded from entitlement to SPC (mixed age
couples); various minor amendments made.

Chapter 45: IB, SDA and IS, claims and reassessment
Amendments expand the existing guidance on the disregard of pension payments.




Please note: As stated in the October 2019 DMG amendment package we are no
longer providing amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) for each DMG
Volume. This summary of changes document does, however, inform the user of the
significant changes that have taken place in each amended chapter by volume. The
page footers on each page also continue to show the volume, amendment number
and date.

From June 2020 page footers will no longer appear. This is because the DMG
chapters on GOV.UK will be produced from the Department’s ‘Intranet version’ of the
DMG. This is an internet based product and as such there are no ‘book’ pages and
associated footers.
