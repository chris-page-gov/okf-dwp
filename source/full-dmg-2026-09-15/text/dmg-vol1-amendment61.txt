Amended Chapters for June 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 1: Decision making and appeals

Chapter 1: Principles of Decision Making and Evidence
The changes enhance the guidance on evidence by adding paragraphs on
“Relationship breakdown” (01322) and “Contacting third parties” (01323) as well as
making some consequential amendments.


Chapter 2: Claims
Incorporates DMG Memo 01/20 at paragraph 02004.




Please note that DMG volume amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) are
