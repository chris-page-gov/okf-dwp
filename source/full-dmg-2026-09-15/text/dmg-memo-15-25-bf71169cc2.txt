Introduction


    1. This memo provides guidance on the Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025 1 (BSAI
      Act), which came into force on 2.12.2025.


                                               1 Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Act 2025, ch 31


    2. This legislation confrms:


             1. which European Union (EU) nationals are treated as having rights under the Withdrawal

               Agreement1 (WA),


             2. which European Economic Area (EEA) and Swiss nationals are treated as having rights

               under the relevant separation agreements2,


             3. the family members treated as in scope of 2.13 and 2.24.


 1 UK-EU Withdrawal Agreement 2 EEA EFTA separation agreement, Swiss Rights Agreement 3 UK-EU
                    Withdrawal Agreement 4 EEA EFTA separation agreement, Swiss Rights Agreement


Section 45


    3. Section 451 means that all EU other EEA and Swiss nationals who resided in the UK prior to the
      end of the transition period, and their family members, with leave to enter or remain in the UK
      granted under the EU Settlement Scheme (EUSS) will be treated as a benefciary under the WA or

      the relevant separation agreement2.


       1 BSAI Act 2025 2UK-EU Withdrawal Agreement, EEA EFTA separation agreement, Swiss Rights
                                                                                                 Agreement,


    4. This includes individuals who were not within personal scope of that Agreement, because they
       were not residing in the UK in accordance with EU law immediately before the end of the
       transition period at 11pm on 31 December 2020.


    5. Where a person has been granted EU Settlement Scheme (EUSS) status and can now access

       DWP benefts as a result of section 45 of the BSAI Act 20251, the Home Offce will have decided
       that the person meets the requirements of the EUSS. DWP are expected to rely on that decision
       for as long as the person continues to hold EUSS status.


                                                                                       1 s.45 BSAI Act 2025


    6. The BSAI Act should be applied to any decision made on or after 02.12.2025.


Impact on decision making


    7. Once the act is in force, an assessment on whether a person is in scope of Article 10 WA (or
       equivalent) will no longer be required by DWP.


                                                          1 DMG Volume 02, paragraphs 073496-073499


    8. In addition to the above, paragraphs 39-40 of the DMG Memo 26/20 1 should be disregarded and
       will no longer be considered the correct interpretation.


1 26/20: Right to Reside – The Citizens' Rights (Application Deadline and Temporary Protection) (EU Exit)
                                                                   Regulations 2020 – IS, JSA, ESA & SPC


    9. This change does not impact establishing BAU Habitual Residence Test requirements. An
       individual has to still demonstrate a qualifying right to reside at the date of claim to income-
       related benefts.


Relevant benefts impacted


    10.Section 45 will impact decision making in the following benefts:


    • Universal Credit,


    • Housing Beneft,


    • State Pension Credit
