INTRODUCTION
1. This memo gives guidance on The Scotland Act 2016 (Social Security) (Adult Disability Payment and

Child Disability Payment) (Amendment) Regulations 2022, which come into force on 21. 03. 221.


                            1 Scotland Act 2016 (SS) (ADP and CDP) (Amdts) Regs 2022; SI 2021 No.335



CHANGES

2. The Scottish Government (SG) is due to introduce Adult Disability Payment (ADP) 1 from March 2022.
This is to replace PIP for working age adults resident in Scotland.


                                                                                  1 DAWAP(S)Regs 2022


3. The changes


  1. prevent payment of AA and DLA if ADP is in payment and


  2. in cases of cross-border movement from Scotland to another part of the UK, for a claimant who has
reached state pension age where they make a claim for AA, this will start where entitlement is
assessed and confirmed, after the 13 week ADP run-on payment has ended.
PREVENTING PAYMENT OF AA
4. The legislation provides that a person shall not be entitled to AA while they are entitled to ADP in order

to prevent duplicate payment of the two benefits1. As a result, ADP will be listed as an incompatible
benefit with AA under appendix 1 of DMG paragraph 17005.


                                        1 Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 s 64(1A)(aa)



PREVENTING PAYMENT OF DLA
5. The legislation provides that a person shall not be entitled to DLA while they are entitled to ADP in

order to prevent duplicate payment of the two benefits1. As a result, ADP will be listed as an
incompatible benefit with DLA under appendix 1 of DMG paragraph 17005.


                                              1 Social Security Contributions and Benefits Act 1992 s 71(7)



CROSS BORDER MOVEMENT FROM SCOTLAND TO ANOTHER PART
OF THE UK
6. A claimant who


1. is a recipient of ADP and


2. subsequently moves from Scotland to another part of the UK


retains entitlement to ADP for a period of 13 weeks beginning with the date of the move from

Scotland1.


                    1 Disability Assistance for Working Age People (Scotland) Regulations 2022, reg 53(1)


7. If the circumstances in paragraph 6 apply, claimants are to be treated as


   1. ordinarily resident in Scotland and


   2. not habitually resident in the United Kingdom, the Republic of Ireland, the Isle of Man or the
Channel Islands


   for that period of 13 weeks1.
