Amended Chapters for June 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 8: Employment and Support Allowance

Chapter 43: Membership of the household
Amendment made to the definition of a couple at 43006, and legal ref, following SI
2019/1458.

Chapter 44: Normal amount payable and components
Memo DMG 02/20 incorporated at Note to 44076 (“In receipt of, or entitled to, a

Memo 08/20 incorporated at Appendix 2 – parental bereavement leave.
