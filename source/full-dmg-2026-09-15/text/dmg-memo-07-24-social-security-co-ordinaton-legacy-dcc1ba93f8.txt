Background
4. From 1.1.21, individuals and their family members moving between the UK, Ireland, Gibraltar,
Switzerland, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway, who are not covered by the


    1. EU-UK-WA or


    2. UK-EEA EFTA Separation Agreement or


    3. UK-Swiss Citizens’ Rights Agreement


 can no longer rely on protections under EU law for social security co-ordination.


5. The UK has made reciprocal arrangements with


      1. Ireland1
      2. Gibraltar2


      3. Switzerland3


      4. Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway4


which gives effect to social security co-ordination between the UK and those countries from the dates
as shown at paragraph 2 of this memo.


                                                       1 SS (Ireland) Order 2019; 2 SS (Gibraltar) Order 2024
                        3 SS (Switzerland) Order 2021; 4 SS (Iceland) (Liechtenstein) (Norway) Order 2023




6. The Trade & Co-operation Agreement1 [TCA] social security protocol provides for social security co-
ordination between EU Member States and the UK from the end of the transition period on 31.12.20 and
applied from 1.1.21 for those not in scope of the EU-UK-WA.


                                                                                                        1TCA



Ireland

7. The Schedule to the 2019 Order1 broadly replicates existing legislation2 ensuring that there is no
change in the social security rights of British nationals, Irish nationals and their family members moving
between the UK and Ireland as a result of the UK’s withdrawal from the EU.


                                                   1 SS (Ireland) Order 2019; 2 Regulation (EC) No 883/2004


8. When making a claim for long-term care benefits from the UK, persons moving from


      1. the UK to Ireland are able to benefit from the broader legal test of whether they can show a
      ‘genuine and sufficient link’ to the UK or


      2. Ireland to the UK, the narrower ‘past-presence’ legal test is applied (see Note).


Note: Periods of insurance in Ireland will be applied to said persons’ when the PPT is applied to them. For
full guidance on the PPT see DMG Chapter 07.


9. When a person in receipt of a pension from both the UK and Ireland makes a claim for


      1. sickness benefit or


      2. maternity and paternity benefits or


      3. long term care benefits
      the competent state for the payment of the benefit is the state the person has been subject to the
      legislation the longest.


10. The social security rights of UK and Irish nationals provided for under the Common Travel Area

agreement are unchanged by the 2019 Order1.


                                                                                  1 SS (Ireland) Order 2019



Gibraltar
11. For persons not in scope of the


      1. EU-UK-WA or


      2. UK-EEA EFTA Separation Agreement or


      3. UK-Swiss Citizens’ Rights Agreement or


      4. UK-Swiss Social Security Convention


      the Schedule to the 2024 Order1 replicates existing legislation2 ensuring that there is no change to
      the social security rights of individuals and their family members moving between the UK and
      Gibraltar as a result of the UK’s withdrawal from the EU.


                                            1 SS (Gibraltar) Order 2024; 2 Regulation (EC) No 883/2004;


12. The 2024 Order1 came into effect on 1.6.24. To ensure continuity, claims and awards made


      1. for periods on or before 31.12.20 will be determined under the earlier agreement 2, which

      provided that previous legislation3 applied or


      2. from 1.1.21 will be determined under the 2024 Order1. However, Article 65 (3) does not permit
      claims to be backdated between 1.6.24 to 1.1.21 (i.e. no award can be backdated between those
      two dates).


                                 1 SS (Gibraltar) Order 2024; 2 Exchange of letters (Gibraltar) Order 1974;


                                                                          3 Regulation (EC) No 883/2004



Switzerland

13. The Schedule to the 2021 Order1 has fewer or modified social security rights and applies to all
individuals and their family members moving between the UK and Switzerland with the following
exceptions,


      1. for Benefits in Kind, only Swiss, UK and EU nationals (plus stateless persons and refugees) are in
      scope for UK healthcare where the UK is competent


      2. only Swiss, UK and EU nationals (plus stateless persons and refugees) are in scope for Swiss
      cash benefits and healthcare where Switzerland is competent.


Note: See paragraph 15 for a further explanation of the changes to social security rights under the 2021
Order.


                                                                             1SS (Switzerland) Order 2021



Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein

14. The Schedule to the 20231 Order has fewer or modified social security rights for individuals and their
family members moving between the UK, Iceland, Norway, and Liechtenstein. See paragraph 15 for a
further explanation of those changes.


                                                       1 SS (Iceland) (Liechtenstein) (Norway) Order 2023



Changes

15. For Switzerland, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway, social security co-ordination under the TCA 1 and

the 2021 Order2 and 2023 Order3 differ from the rights that exist for persons covered by the EU-UK-WA.


                1 TCA; 2 SS (Switzerland) Order 2021; 3 SS (Iceland) (Liechtenstein) (Norway) Order 2023


16. The rules for aggregation and exportability continue to apply to State Retirement Pension.


17. The rules for the exporting of


  1. WMA


  2. WPA and


  3. WP


  continue to apply.


18. New claims may be made for WP for deaths occurring prior to 6.4.17.


19. Exporting is not allowable for an award of


  1. AA
  2. CA


  3. DLA (Care)


  4. WFP (see Note 1)


  unless the claimant is within scope of the EU-UK-WA.



Note 1: For WFP the claimant must be within the full scope of the EU-UK-WA.


Note 2: For guidance on the differences between how social security benefits are co-ordinated under
the TCA and the EU-UK-WA see DMG memo 11/22 and Chapter 7, Part 2, Appendix 3 of the DMG.



Interim provisions
20. From 1.1.21 for those who were not in scope of the Separation Agreements, only pensions were
coordinated. This means that for claimants between 1.1.21 and 31.12.23 there were less rights but this
was temporary pending the new agreement and only applied to those who were not covered by the
Separation Agreements.


Note: The Separation Agreements are shown at paragraph 5 and are listed 1-4.



Norway

21. From the end of the transition period on 31.12.20 to the entry into force of the 2023 Order 1 on 1.1.24,
the UK and Norway applied the 1991 Agreement between them as amended by 2020 Exchange of

Letters. These were implemented by the 2020 Order2.


                  1 SS (Iceland) (Liechtenstein) (Norway) Order 2023; 2 SS (Norway) Order 2020 No. 1597



Switzerland

22. After the end of the transition period until the entry into force of the Agreement on 1.11.21 1, the UK
and Switzerland used the historic 1968 Convention between the two countries to coordinate social
security.
