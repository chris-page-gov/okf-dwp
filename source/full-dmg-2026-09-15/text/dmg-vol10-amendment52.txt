Amended Chapters for June 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 10: Benefits for incapacity, disability, maternity
and bereavement

Chapter 62: Maternity Benefits
The amendments incorporate memos DMG 08/20 and 09/20. They also make minor
and consequential changes. Abbreviations “PBL - Parental Bereavement Leave”
and “PBP - Parental Bereavement Pay” are also introduced.




Please note that DMG volume amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) are
no longer produced. This summary of changes document does, however, inform the
user of the significant changes that have taken place in each amended chapter by
volume. Page footers continue to show the volume, amendment number and date.
