INTRODUCTION

1. This Memo gives guidance on the treatment of Scottish Child Payments
   introduced by The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Information-Sharing and
   Scottish Child Payment) (Consequential Provision and Modifications) Order 2020
   (SI 2020/482), (SSSA (CPM) Order). The Order comes into force on 9.11.201.

                                                        1 SSSA (CPM) Order, article 1


BACKGROUND

2. The Scottish Government has introduced a system of Scottish Child Payments.
   Initially, payment will be £10.00 per week to low income families for each eligible
   child, with no cap on the number of children in families. Payments will be monthly.
   Applications, payments and disputes will be administered by Social Security
   Scotland.


TREATMENT OF PAYMENTS FOR SPC

3. Scottish Child Payments are not prescribed as income for the purposes of the
   assessment of SPC1. Scottish Child Payments are disregarded as capital for the
   purpose of calculation income for SPC2.

                   1 SPC Act 02, s 15; SPC Regs, reg 15; 2 SPC Regs, Sch V(20(2)(u))
TREATMENT OF PAYMENTS FOR IS, JSA(IB) & ESA(IR)

4. Scottish Child Payments are disregarded as income1 and as capital2 in the
    assessment of IS, JSA(IB) and ESA(IR).
