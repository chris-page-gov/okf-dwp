INTRODUCTION

      1. This memo gives guidance on the changes to the underlying entitlement conditions for
      Widowed Parent’s Allowance due to the introduction of The Bereavement Benefits (Remedial)

      Order 20231 (See Memo DMG 2-23). These changes apply to surviving spouses and civil partners
      as well as surviving cohabiting partners.


                                            1 Bereavement Benefits (Remedial) Order 2023, SI 2023/134



      BACKGROUND

      2. The Bereavement Benefits (Remedial) Order 2023 came into force on 9.2.23. The Remedial
      Order extends eligibility for WPA to surviving cohabiting partners with dependent children, who
      were not in a legal union with their deceased cohabiting partner on the date of their death.


      3. Before the introduction of the Remedial Order, surviving partners (who were married or in a civil
      partnership with the deceased) in receipt of WPA that entered a cohabiting partnership would
      have     their payments suspended however they would retain underlying entitlement to WPA. If
      their        subsequent cohabiting partnership ended, as long as they continue to satisfy the
      qualifying   conditions, WPA would be payable to them again (See DMG 63037).
