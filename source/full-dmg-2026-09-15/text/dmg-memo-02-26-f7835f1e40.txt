introduction

1. This memo contains details about the income tax and the Social Security contribution rates for
2026/2027 and the increase in some IS/JSA/ESA/SPC benefit rates from 06.04.26. The rates are
introduced by the Social Security Benefits Uprating Order 2026 (SI 2026/148), the Social Security
(Contributions) (Limits and Thresholds, National Insurance Funds Payments and Extension of Veterans
Relief) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/231) and the Social Security Benefit Uprating Regulations 2026 (SI
2026/218. This memo also contains guidance on the Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order 2026
(SI 2026/310) and the Social Security (Contributions)(Re-rating) Consequential Amendment Regulations
2026 (SI 2026/283) which come into effect from 06.04.26.


UPRATING


Benefit rates

2. Any new rates of IS/JSA/ESA/SPC will apply from the first day of the first benefit week which
commences on or after 06.04.26. DMs can find the new rates in the benefit uprating schedule and
benefit desk aids and should bear them in mind when reading examples in the ADM that contain benefit
rates.


Housing costs non-dependant contribution – IS/JSA/ESA/SPC
