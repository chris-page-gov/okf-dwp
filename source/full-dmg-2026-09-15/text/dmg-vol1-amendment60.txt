                  Decision Makers Guide
Amended Chapters for February 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 1: Decision making and appeals

Chapter 2: Claims
Paragraphs 02378 (sure start maternity grants) and 02379 (funeral payments)
added, paragraph 02543 (SF funeral and maternity payments) amended.

Chapter 3: Revision
Incorporates Memo DMG 5/19 (Late application for a mandatory reconsideration),
includes guidance at 03241 – 03247 about changes to collection of Class 2 NI
contributions, makes minor changes to correct cross references and examples that
no longer apply and signposts to the new Annex F (Late application for a Mandatory
Reconsideration (MR)).

Chapter 4: Supersession, suspension and termination
Minor amendment made at paragraph 04226.

Chapter 6: Making appeals and staying
Amendments made to reflect the decision in CDLA/998/2017 (which now reported
as [2018] AACR 33), in respect of where a claimant with an appointee is not
precluded from bringing an appeal independently (DMG 06051) and the power the
First-tier Tribunal have in appointing a litigation friend (DMG 06428).

Annex F: Late application for a Mandatory Reconsideration (MR)
Addition of this new annex.




Please note: As stated in the October 2019 DMG amendment package we are no
longer providing amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) for each DMG
Volume. This summary of changes document does, however, inform the user of the
significant changes that have taken place in each amended chapter by volume. The
page footers on each page also continue to show the volume, amendment number
and date.

From June 2020 page footers will no longer appear. This is because the DMG
chapters on GOV.UK will be produced from the Department’s ‘Intranet version’ of the
DMG. This is an internet based product and as such there are no ‘book’ pages and
associated footers.
