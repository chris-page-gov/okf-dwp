3.    Amendment 51 affects chapters 20, 23; The changes

      • DMG Chapter 20 amendments to clarify “staying put” arrangements and
      new requirements for 16 to 18 years olds once school leaving age is reached.
      • DMG Chapter 23 adds new guidance on deductions from JSA(C) for
      pension payments.

        Example

        Julie is 18 and is estranged from her parents and has been receiving IS while she
        was in relevant education. She leaves school on 6.7.11 so the terminal date in her
        case is 31.8.11. The terminal date falls in the CHB week 29.8.11 to 4.9.11 so Julie is
        a qualifying young person until 4.9.11. Her entitlement to IS will end when the IS
        week in which she ceased to be a qualifying young person and therefore ceased to
        be in that specific prescribed category of person (subject to her not satisfying any of
        the other prescribed categories) ends. Her BWE day is Wednesday so the benefit
        week in which Julie ceased to be a qualifying young person is 1.9.11 to 7.9.11. Her
        IS entitlement would therefore end on 7.9.11.
