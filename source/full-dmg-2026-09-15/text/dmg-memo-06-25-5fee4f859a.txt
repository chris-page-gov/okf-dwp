INTRODUCTION
1. The purpose of this memo is to give guidance to decision makers (DM) on The Social Security

(Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance) (Consequential Amendments) Order 2025 1. The amendments
will come into force on 21.3.25.


                                                                                          1 SI 2025 No. 227



BACKGROUND

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect the introduction by the Scottish
Government (SG) of the Scottish Adult Disability Living Allowance (SADLA). SADLA will


      1. replace DLA for current DLA claimants who are over the age of 18 and are resident in Scotland
      and


      2. operate broadly the same way as DLA so those in receipt will have the same access to additional
      amounts and exemptions in reserved benefits as those in receipt of DLA.


                                                      1 SS(Scot) Act 2018 (DA)(Consq Amdts) Order 2025

BACKDATING CLAIMS FOR A RELEVANT BENEFIT

Qualifying benefit
36. For the purposes of the rules on backdating claims for a relevant benefit after the award of a

qualifying benefit in DMG 02260, SADLA is a “qualifying benefit” 1 from 21.3.25.


                                                                            1 SS (C&P) Regs, reg 6(38)(b)(v)


New backdating rule for SADLA
37. After 28 days in hospital or a care home, the amount of SADLA reduces to nil. where


      1. as a result of the SADLA reducing to nil, an award of a relevant benefit (DMG 02263) has been
      terminated or reduced, or payment has ceased; and


      2. SADLA is subsequently restored


             2.1. on revision, supersession, or appeal or


             2.2. because the claimant has left hospital or the care home and


      3. the relevant benefit is claimed again within three months of the date on which a decision to
      restore SADLA was made.
The new claim for the relevant benefit can be treated as made on the date in paragraph 38 1.


                                                                                  1 SS (C&P) Regs, Reg 6(19)


38. Where the provisions in paragraph 37 are satisfied, the new claim for the relevant benefit is treated
as made on


      1. the date from which the previous award of the relevant benefit was terminated or


      2. the date from which SADLA increases to more than nil


whichever is the later.




REVISION AND SUPERSESSION

Definition of Scottish disability benefit
39. The definition of ‘Scottish disability benefit’ for revision and supersession purposes 1 has been
amended so that it now includes SADLA. See further guidance in paragraphs 40 to 48.


                                                                                1 SS CS (D&A) Regs, reg 1(3)


Supersession of a SPC award because of entitlement to SADLA
40. Where


      1. an award of SPC is superseded on the ground that there has been a relevant change of
      circumstances since the award took effect and


      2. the change of circumstances is that the claimant or partner has become entitled to SADLA


the superseding decision takes effect from the beginning of the benefit week in which the first day of
the new SADLA entitlement falls or, if that is not practicable, on the first day of the following benefit

week1.


                                                             1 SS CS (D&A) Regs, Sch 3B, paras 2(b)(v) & (vi)

41. Where


      1. the DM awards entitlement to a relevant benefit (DMG Annex A) and


      2. the claimant or a member of his family is awarded SADLA for a period that includes the date
      from which the relevant benefit has been awarded
the award of the relevant benefit can be revised. 1
