                  Decision Makers Guide
Amended Chapters for February 2020 - Summary of changes

Volume 3: Subjects common to all benefits

Chapter 17: Claims

Incorporates Memo DMG 11/16 about changes to overlapping benefit rules
following the introduction of State Pension, and makes a minor amendment to
include new style ESA and new style JSA in the personal benefits affected by the
overlapping benefit rules.




Please note: As stated in the October 2019 DMG amendment package we are no
longer providing amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) for each DMG
Volume. This summary of changes document does, however, inform the user of the
significant changes that have taken place in each amended chapter by volume. The
page footers on each page also continue to show the volume, amendment number
and date.

From June 2020 page footers will no longer appear. This is because the DMG
chapters on GOV.UK will be produced from the Department’s ‘Intranet version’ of the
DMG. This is an internet based product and as such there are no ‘book’ pages and
associated footers.
