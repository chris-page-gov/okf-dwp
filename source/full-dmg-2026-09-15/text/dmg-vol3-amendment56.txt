16222   Where the claimant's partner had earnings of £235 or more in the benefit week
        before that in which the increase is to be paid, no increase will be paid for the first
        child or qualifying young person1. After this, the rule operates in steps of £31. For
        each multiple of £31 by which the earnings exceed £235 , the increase for a further
        child or qualifying young person will not be payable2.

        Earnings for less than a week

16224   When considering the earnings rule the DM should consider the total earnings in
        the previous week. Where the claimants partner does not work for a full week, the
        earnings from the days worked may still affect the whole benefit to be paid in the
        next week. If that person works only one day but on that day earns more than the
        prescribed weekly amount, no increase is paid in the next week.

        Occupational and personal pensions

16225   Payments of occupational and personal pension are taken into account when
        calculating earnings unless savings provisions apply (see DMG Chapter 15).
