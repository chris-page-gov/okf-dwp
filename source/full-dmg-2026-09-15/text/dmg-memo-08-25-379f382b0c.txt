INTRODUCTION

    1. This memo gives guidance on the Social Security (Income and Capital Disregards) (Amendment) (No. 2)
       Regulations 20251 [SS (I&C) (Amdt) Regs 2025], SI 2025/778. These changes take effect from 22.07.25.

       The changes extend to England, Wales and Scotland.


                                                                                                       1 SI 2025/778




BACKGROUND

    2. Existing regulations1 are amended to include payments of miscarriage of justice compensation as
       payments which are to be disregarded as income and capital for the purposes of calculating entitlement to
       income-related benefits.
                                                                          1 IS (Gen) Regs 1987, reg 2(1); JSA Regs 1996, reg 1(3);


                                                                                 SPC Regs 2002, reg 1(2); ESA Regs 2008, reg 2(1)


DEFINITIONS

   3. These regulations1 insert new definitions for


     1. miscarriage of justice compensation payments (see paragraph 4) and


     2. qualifying person (see paragraph 5).

                                                                                              1 SS (I & C) Amdt (No.2) Regs)2025


Miscarriage of justice

   4. A miscarriage of justice compensation payment means a payment made under relevant legislation 1 or any
      other payment made by the Secretary of State, the Scottish Ministers or, in Northern Ireland, the

      Department of Justice for the purpose of compensating a person for


     1. a miscarriage of justice in criminal proceedings or


     2. being wrongfully charged with a criminal offence2.


                                                                               1 CJA 1988 s.133(1);2 IS (Gen) Regs 1987, reg 2(1);
                                                         JSA Regs 1996, reg 1(3); SPC Regs 2002, reg 1(2); ESA Regs 2008, reg 2(1)


Qualifying person

   5. For the definition of qualifying person, a miscarriage of justice compensation payment is inserted into
      relevant legislation1. This means any person that receives a payment from these schemes is added to the

      definition of a qualifying person.


                                                                          1 IS (Gen) Regs 1987, reg 2(1); JSA Regs 1996, reg 1(3);


                                                                                 SPC Regs 2002, reg 1(2); ESA Regs 2008, reg 2(1)

7. For IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR) and SPC any miscarriage of justice compensation payment is disregarded indefinitely
   if the payment is made


     1. by or on behalf of the person who got the money from the scheme and that person


            1.1 is a qualifying person or


            1.2. was a qualifying person if the person has died and


     2. to or for the benefit of


            2.1. a partner or former partner of the person who got the money from the schemes and they are not

             estranged, divorced or part of a dissolved civil partnership or were not if the person has died 1 or


            2.2. a child or young person who


                     2.2a. is a member of the family of the person who got the money from the schemes or


                     2.2b. was a member of that person’s family and is a member of the claimant’s family 2.

9. For IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR) and SPC any miscarriage of justice compensation payment is disregarded

      1. from the date of payment until two years after the date of death of the person if the payment is made by

      or on behalf of the person who received the payment 1 or


      2. for two years from the date of death if the payment is made from the estate of that person 2 where the
      payment was made to a person who


             2.1 is a qualifying person and


             2.2 has no partner or former partner from whom the person is not estranged, divorced or part of a
             dissolved civil partnership or


             2.3 is a child who is or has been a member of the person’s household and the payment was made to


                    2.3a the person’s parent or step-parent or


                    2.3b a person standing in place of the parent, if there is no parent or step-parent, and at the
                    date of the payment the person is a child or student who has not finished FTE.

NOTIONAL CAPITAL


10. For IS1, JSA(IB)2 & ESA(IR)3, any miscarriage of justice compensation payment paid to



     1. a third party for the claimant or their partner or
      2. the claimant or their partner for a third party



   is not to be treated as notional capital.

INCOME DISREGARDS

11. Fully disregard1 any


       1. miscarriage of justice compensation payment in IS, JSA(IB) and ESA(IR) and


       2. income from capital that was a miscarriage of justice compensation payment in IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR).


                                                             1 IS (Gen) Regs, Sch 9, paras 39(1A) to 39(6)   ; JSA Regs, Sch 7, para 41(1A);


                                                                                                    ESA Regs, Sch 8 para 41(1A) to 41(6)




12. Miscarriage of justice compensation payments are also disregarded for the following purposes


       1. payments to a Third Party in respect of a claimant or their partner 1


       2. non-dependants’ gross weekly income for the purposes of calculating deductions for allowable housing

       costs2


       3. income treated as capital3.
