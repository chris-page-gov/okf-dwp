Chapter 23
DMG Memos 15/20 and 15/21 incorporated – payments disregarded when
considering the income of non-dependants (see para. 23775).

DMG Memos 09/21 and 03/22 incorporated – when considering whether a non-
dependant deduction for housing costs is appropriate, or when calculating their
gross weekly income, CDP and ADP are added to the list of benefits to consider
(paras. 23770, 23775).

DMG Memo 3/22 incorporated – ADP is added to the list of qualifying benefits
when considering entitlement to premiums etc. (paras. 23103, 23106, 23122,
23123, 23127, 23182, 23202, 23220, 23243, 23292).

DMG Memo 12/22 incorporated – ADP commences/converted from PIP/DLA
whilst a claimant is in hospital – entitlement to SDP will have to be considered
(para. 23289).

Up-rating DMG Memos 4/21, 7/22 and 4/23 incorporated (Appendix 4).

References to DMG Memo 06/22 removed – no changes to definition of
terminally ill needed to paras. 23103 and 23123.

December 2020 updates

Chapter 23
DMG 23225 gives guidance on the Shared Lives Scheme when considering the
SDP and non-dependants. The Note to this paragraph has been amended to
make clear just who is or is not a non-dependant, especially in relation to the
carer's own household.
