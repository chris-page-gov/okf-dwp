    INTRODUCTION

1   This memo provides guidance on the Social Security (Miscellaneous Amendment
    No.5) Regulations 2017 (SI 2017/1187). These regulations come into force on
    6.12.181.

                                               1 The Social Security (Misc Amdt No.5) Regs 2017, reg 1(b).


    BACKGROUND

2   This SI makes a number of minor amendments to relevant legislation in advance of
    the equalisation of pensionable age for men and women1.

                                                                        1 Pensions Act 1995, Sch 4, Part 1


    HOME INCOME PLANS – IS, JSA, ESA AND SPC

3   These regulations amend the current provision that payments from home income
    plans (provided by an annuity secured on the home) may be disregarded in IS, JSA,
    SPC and ESA awards where, at the time the loan was made to the person, the person
    was aged 65. From 6.12.18, if the loan was made to a person who is

    1.    aged 65 or

    2.    if it was higher at that time, pensionable age
    the same disregard may be applied, subject to all the other conditions being met 1.

     1 IS (Gen) Regs, sch 9, para 17(c), JSA Regs, sch 7, para 18(c), SPC Regs, sch IV, para 10(b), ESA Regs, sch 8, para
                                                                                                                   18(c).


    SUPERSESSION – NON DEPENDANTS

4   From 6.12.18 the reference in DMG 04660, 04662 and 04670 to the claimant or their
    partner being aged 65 or over is removed1.

                                                                             1 SS & CS (D&A) Regs 1999, reg 7(17B)(b)


    SPC

    Non-dependant deductions

5   A non-dependant deduction is not appropriate where the non-dependant is a full time
    student and the claimant or partner has reached the age of 65. From 6.12.18 this
    requirement is amended because, at this point in time, everyone on SPC will be over
    the SPA, consequently there is no need to have the additional provision which could
    apply for SPC claimants under 65 where their non-dep is a student1.

                                                                                 1 SPC Regs, Sch II, Para 14(7)(c)& (cc)
