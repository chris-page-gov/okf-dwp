        Introduction

        Abolition of schemes

73001   WC (Supp) and PB and MDB will not apply from 5.12.121. Therefore, from that date,
        people wishing to claim for

        1.       accidents which happened or

        2.       diseases caused by employment

        before 5.7.48 should claim IIDB under the normal rules2. Apart from DMG 73002 –
        73003, the only guidance being retained is on other compensation (see DMG 73332
        et seq) and death benefit (see DMG 73412 et seq).
                                                                 1 WR Act 12, s 64(2); 2 SS CB Act 92, s 94(1) & 108(1)


        People receiving WC (Supp) or PB and MDB

73002   People receiving WC (Supp) or PB and MDB before 5.12.12

        1.       transferred to IIDB and

        2.       received benefit at the corresponding rates1

        from 5.12.12.

        Note: People who received lesser incapacity allowance or partial disablement
        allowance were paid a higher rate of benefit from 5.12.12 under the corresponding
        rates.
                             1 Industrial Injuries Benefit (Injuries Arising Before 5 July 1948) Regulations, reg 2 & Sch

        before 5.12.12

73003   Where a claim for WC (Supp) or PB and MDB is made but not determined before
        5.12.12, the DM should treat that claim as a claim for IIDB1.

        Entitlement to other compensation
73332   The third condition for entitlement to disablement allowance is that the claimant
        must never have received, nor be entitled to receive compensation for the disease
        under

        1.      WC legislation1 or

        2.      any compensation scheme or law outside GB2 (other than compensation in
                respect of total or partial incapacity for work under

                2.1   specified legislation3 or

                2.2   by virtue of any scheme or law in force in any country or territory
                      outside GB providing for compensation in respect of the disease.
                                      1 Workmen’s Compensation Acts and Schemes; 2 PB & MDB Scheme, art 3(1)(d);
                        3 Refractories Industries (Silicosis) Scheme 1931 & Sandstone Industry (Silicosis) Scheme 1931


73333   A person who has been awarded WC for the disease is excluded from PB and MDB
        even though no compensation was received because, for example

        1.      an employer went bankrupt or

        2.      the person chose to have the compensation commuted to a lump sum.

73334   People who

        1.      have not been awarded WC for the disease and

        2.      still appear to have a valid claim

        are excluded from receiving benefit under PB and MDB unless they can show that
        they are unable to make a successful claim against their employer.

73335   A person is not prevented from receiving a full allowance under PB and MDB if they
        are receiving payment of WC for

        1.      an accident or

        2.      some disease other than that claimed under PB and MDB.

        Note: See DMG 73340 for an exception.


        People totally disabled by accident or disease not
        covered by PB and MDB

73336   People awarded WC for total disablement resulting from an accident or disease
        cannot get further compensation if they later suffer a second accident or disease. As
        they have been totally disabled by the first accident or disease they do not have any
        earning capacity left to lose.

        Certification under workmen’s compensation legislation

73342   A claim to compensation for pneumoconiosis or byssinosis under the WC legislation1
        must be supported by a certificate from the Silicosis (or Byssinosis) Medical Board
        that a claimant is disabled by the disease. There is no such Board for the other
        industrial diseases; claimants should obtain a certificate from a “certifying surgeon”
        that they are suffering from the disease.
                                                                        1 Workmen’s Compensation Acts


73343   In order to qualify for compensation, claimants must

        1.    obtain their certificate (unless excepted because their employer agreed they
              were liable to pay compensation) and

        2.    establish that the disease is due to the nature of any employment in which they
              were employed at any time within the twelve months before the date of
              disablement.

73344   If the disease developed more than twelve months after the claimant had left the
        relevant employment, there is no entitlement to compensation.

73345   When claimants have obtained their certificate, they have still to claim compensation.
        The claim has to be made within six months of the date of development of the
        disease or as soon as may be allowed for reasonable cause.


        Time limit for certification for pneumoconiosis

73346   The time limit for certification under

        1.    all Schemes for pneumoconiosis under WC legislation1, except the Asbestos
              Industry (Asbestos) Scheme 1931 and the Refractories Industries (Silicosis)
              Schemes, was five years from the date of leaving the industry

        2.    the Asbestos Scheme was three years

        3.    the Refractories Scheme was three years unless the claimant did not again
              become employed in an industry with a silicosis risk. In such a case there was
              no time limit.
                                                                        1 Workmen’s Compensation Acts

73347   Benefit under PB and MDB is not payable1 if the claimant, representative or any
        relative of the claimant receives damages whether

        1.      at common law or

        2.      under specified legislation2.
             1 PB & MDB Scheme, art 3(1)(f); 2 Fatal Accidents Act 1976; Law Reform (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1934


73348   Where a weekly allowance is involved, a payment of damages will always have an
        effect. If damages have been received before the date of claim an allowance is not
        payable. If a weekly allowance is in payment the award should be allowed to
        continue up until the date when the claimant or representative recovers damages.
        The decision should then be superseded because of a relevant change of
        circumstances and terminated.


        Damages and death benefit

73349   If death benefit is involved, the benefit is not payable if the damages are received
        before the claim is made.

73350   A payment of damages received after the date of claim has no effect on the payment
        of death benefit. This applies even if the damages are received between the date of
        claim and the date the DM decides the claim.
