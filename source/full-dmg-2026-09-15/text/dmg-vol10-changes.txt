May 2023 updates

Chapter 62
Various amendments as follows:

Note under para 62524 to provide greater clarity within guidance about the Earnings
Test and specifically about the fact that whilst a woman doesn’t have to have received
a payment in each of the chosen 13 weeks within her Test Period, they need to have
earned an amount equal to the MAT (when averaged) for each of those 13 weeks –
irrespective of when the payment is made.

Para. 62017 amended to give example of certificate of confinement (MATB1) and para.
62020 changed to clarify the types of evidence required for stillbirth.

Note 2 added to para. 62516 for clarification regarding zero hours contracts and
agency workers.

Amendment to paras. 62540 and 62541 to show that for women who have not paid
their NICS to qualify for the standard rate of MA the legislation does clearly indicate
that they should be treated as receiving earnings equal to the MAT (£30) for that week.
At present the fact that this relates to each individual week isn’t clear.

Para. 62524 – legislative reference amended from ‘SS (MA) (Earnings) Regs, reg 6(1)’
to ‘SS (MA) (Earnings) Regs, reg 6(1)(b)’. Additional note added “Note: The position
differs for the self-employed (see DMG 62540)”.

Para 62541 – legislative reference corrected.

Para. 62544 – legislative reference added.

Added para. 62545 ‘Payment of class 2 contributions treated as paid’ due to legislation
introduced by The Social Security (Class 2 National Insurance Contributions Increase
of Threshold) Regulations 2022.

Numbers changed at paras. 62546 to 62552 due to addition of para. 62545.

Para. 62025 – removal of sentence - “If, as a result of this calculation, disallowance
would be appropriate because the length of pregnancy falls short of a full 24 weeks by
one week or less, the case should be referred to Medical Services for advice.”
