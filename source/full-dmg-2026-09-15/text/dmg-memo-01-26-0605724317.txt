Introduction

1. This memo gives guidance on The Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018 (Carer’s Assistance)

(Consequential Modifications) Order 20261 which came into force on 15.03.26.


                                                                                1 SI 2026 (link is external)



Background

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect additional elements to Scottish
Carer’s Assistance, such as extending the run-on period for which a carer will receive Scottish carer
support after the death of their cared-for person, from the current eight weeks to a period
of twelve weeks, as well as a change in definition for “carer support payment” (CSP).


                                                                                1 SI 2026 (link is external)



Income support

Entitlement to premiums
3. A carer premium continues to be paid for a period of 8 weeks where the person being cared for dies.
However, where the carer premium has been awarded because the claimant or their partner is

entitled a CSP, that period will be 12 weeks1.


                                                                           1 IS Regs 87, Sch 2, Para 14ZA.



Jobseeker's Allowance

Entitlement to premiums
4. A carer premium continues to be paid for a period of 8 weeks where the person being cared for dies.
However, where the carer premium has been awarded because the claimant or their partner is entitled a

State Pension Credit

Additional amount for the severely disabled
5. DMG 78030 and 78610 and DMG Memo 12-23 give guidance that when considering entitlement to
an additional amount for claimants who are severely disabled, no amount is payable in respect of a
claimant or their partner if someone is in receipt of


      1. a CA or


      2. the carer element of UC or


      3. CSP


      in respect of caring for them.


From 15.03.26, CSP means the carer support component of carer support.


Additional amount for carers
6. DMG 78100 and 78620 gives guidance that an additional amount for carers may be awarded for each
claimant who is entitled to CA. From 15.03.26 that also includes those claimants entitled to the carer

support payment component of carer support1.


7. An additional amount for carers continues to be paid for a period of 8 weeks when the person being
cared for dies. From 15.03.26, where that additional amount has been awarded because the claimant is
entitled to the carer support payment component of carer support, that period will be 12 weeks.


                                                                 1 SPC Regs, Sch 1 para 4 (link is external)



Employment and Support Allowance

Entitlement to premiums
8. A carer premium continues to be paid for a period of 8 weeks where the person being cared for dies.
However, where the carer premium has been awarded because the claimant or their partner is entitled
to a CSP, that period will be 12 weeks.


                                                                   1 ESA Regs 08, Sch 4, Reg 8(2a) & (4).



Deferring Entitlement to State Pension/Retirement Pension

Lump Sum Payments
9. DMG 75405 et seq provides guidance on people entitled to a lump sum payment when they defer their
RP. The amount of RP which would have been payable had it not been deferred does not include any
pension, where for all of an accrual period, a person has been in receipt of the carer support

payment component of carer support1.


                                                                           1 SS (Def RP) Regs, reg 3(1)(a)(v)


Increments
10. DMG 75349 et seq provides guidance on people entitled to increments when they defer
their retirement pension (RP). DMG 74501 et seq provides guidance on people entitled to increments
when they defer their State Pension (SP). When a person is deferring their


      1. RP1 or


      2. SP2


they will not be entitled to increments for any day in a period of deferment when they are in receipt
of the carer support payment component of carer support.




                                                 1 SS (WB & RP) Regs, reg 4(1)(b)(v); 2 SP Regs, reg 11(2)(a)

Engaged in Caring

      1. before they become entitled to the carer support payment component of carer support1


      2. after they cease to be entitled to the carer support payment component of carer support2

benefits disregarded for capital purposes in ESA(IR), IS, JSA(IB), and SPC 1.

Income other than Earnings

14. For IS, JSA and ESA(IR)1 Scottish carer supplement and carer additional person payment given in

accordance with legislation2 are fully disregarded in the calculation of income other than earnings.




Note: Scottish carer supplement and carer additional person payment are not prescribed income for
SPC.




                  1 IS Regs 87, Sch 9, para 87; JSA Regs 96, Sch 7, Para 83; ESA Regs 08, Sch 8, Para 75.



Definitions

15. Where “carer support payment” is defined within legislation1, “carer’s assistance” can be substituted
by “the carer support payment component of carer support”.
