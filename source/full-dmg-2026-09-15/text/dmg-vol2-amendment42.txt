                  Decision Makers Guide
Amended Chapters for February 2020 - Summary of changes
Volume 2: International subjects
Chapter 7 Part 3: Habitual residence and right to reside -
IS/JSA/SPC/ESA
Incorporates Memos DMG 2/16 - Right to reside - IS, JSA(IB), ESA(IR) & SPC - effect
of UT decision; 8/18 - Undocumented Commonwealth Citizens Resident in the UK-JSA,
ESA, IS & SPC; 6/19 - Right to reside - EU Settlement Scheme (EUSS)-IS, JSA, ESA &
SPC; 11/19 Right To Reside - IS, JSA, ESA & SPC and 17/19 Right to reside - EEA
National who ceases self-employment because of the physical constraints of the late
stages of pregnancy and the aftermath of childbirth – IS;

amendments to cross references at 073056, 217, 220-223, 230 & 434;

reference to “temporary admission” at 073185, 073187 & 073195 amended to read
“immigration bail” – implements changes to Sch 10 of the Immigration Act 2016 from
April 2019;

‘Note’ added to 073189 regarding ‘No Recourse to Public Funds’ (NRTPF);

new guidance on Self-employment (Saint Prix) at 073215;

amendment to EDC date in example 2 to paragraph 073226;

amendments to 073254–073260 & 073293-073295 in relation to extended family
members – these amendments implement the CJEU judgment of Banger, which allows
extended family members who meet certain conditions, to benefit from the CJEU ruling
in Surinder Singh;

new guidance on ‘EUSS’ at 073490–073495;

073500, 502, 520, 522, 524, 525, 527 & 529 amended to implement Gubeladze
judgment that A8 extension period was disproportionate and unlawful.


Please note: As stated in the October 2019 DMG amendment package we are no
longer providing amendment packages (‘remove & insert pages’) for each DMG
Volume. This summary of changes document does, however, inform the user of the
significant changes that have taken place in each amended chapter by volume. The
page footers on each page also continue to show the volume, amendment number
and date.

From June 2020 page footers will no longer appear. This is because the DMG
chapters on GOV.UK will be produced from the Department’s ‘Intranet version’ of the
DMG. This is an internet based product and as such there are no ‘book’ pages and
associated footers.
