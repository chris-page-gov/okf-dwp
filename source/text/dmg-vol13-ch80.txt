        General
        Scope of this Chapter

80001   This Chapter contains guidance on transitional provisions1 for SPC. SPC replaces
        IS for claimants who have reached the qualifying age of 602 (see DMG 77032) on or
        before the appointed day.
                                                                                  1 SPC Act 02, s 13; 2 s 1

        Meaning of the appointed day

80002   The appointed day means such day as the Secretary of State may appoint1. The
        appointed day is 6.10.03.
                                                            1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 1(3) & SPC Act 02, s 13(3)

        When do transitional provisions apply

80003   Transitional provisions apply to

        1.    a person who

              1.1    is entitled to IS immediately before 6.10.03 and

              1.2    reaches or has reached the qualifying age of 60 on or before 6.10.031

              (see DMG 80011 et seq)

        2.    claims for SPC made before 6.10.03 and certain claims made within the
              period of 12 months beginning on that day2 (see DMG 80051 et seq)

        3.    the AIP if SPC is awarded from 6.10.033 (see DMG 80071 et seq).
                                                   1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(1); 2 reg 38(1) & 38(4); 3 reg 37
        Meaning of transferee

80004   In this Chapter, transferee1 means any person who is entitled to IS immediately
        before 6.10.03 and who reaches or has reached the qualifying age of 60 on or
        before 6.10.03. A transferee is a person who transfers from IS to SPC.
                                                                                                1 reg 36(1)


        Note: A person in receipt of JSA(IB) does not automatically transfer to SPC.
        Therefore men aged 60 - 64 can decide to continue receiving JSA(IB) rather than
        make a claim for SPC. See DMG 80051 et seq if a person receiving JSA(IB) makes
        a claim for SPC before 6.10.03. See DMG 80064 et seq if a man aged 60 - 64
        continues to receive JSA(IB) and then makes a claim for SPC on or after 6.10.03.

        Transferee treated as making claim for SPC

80011   A transferee will be treated as having made a claim for SPC in the period of six
        months immediately preceding 6.10.031. Where a transferee has been treated as
        having made a claim for SPC before 6.10.03 the DM should try to decide as far as
        practicable the claim before that date2. See DMG 80013 - 80015 if this is not
        possible.
                                                                 1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(2); 2 reg 36(3)


80012   Where DMG 80011 applies, the DM may revise a decision upon application by the
        claimant or a person acting on his behalf. This can happen at any time within the
        period of 13 months beginning on the date on which that decision was notified to the
        claimant1.
                                                                                            1 reg 36(4)


80013   From 6.10.03 people aged 60 or over can no longer receive IS. However, if before
        that date the DM is unable to decide a claim for SPC which a transferee is treated
        as having made, payments may be made as if they were IS. In DMG 80014 - 80015
        such payments are called “IS”.

80014   If the DM is unable to decide a claim for SPC treated as made by a transferee as in
        DMG 800111, any payment made to the transferee

        1.    in respect of a period falling on or after 6.10.03 and

        2.    which would have been payable under an award of IS but for the introduction
              of SPC

        should be offset against any SPC payable under an award on or after 6.10.032 on a
        claim treated as made as in DMG 80011.
                                                                              1 reg 36(10); 2 reg 36(9)

        Example

        Sabrina is 63. She was in receipt of IS immediately before 6.10.03. She is treated
        as having made a claim for SPC but the DM is unable to decide that claim. Sabrina
        continues to receive payments of “IS”. On 20.10.03 the DM decides that Sabrina is
        entitled to SPC. The DM also decides that the amount of “IS” Sabrina has received
        since 6.10.03 is to be offset against the SPC to which she is entitled.

80015   Where a transferee continues to receive payments of “IS” because

        1.    the DM has been unable to decide a claim for SPC and

        2.    the DM then decides that

              2.1    no SPC is payable or



Vol 13 Amendment 14 October 2007
              2.2    the amount of SPC payable is less than the amount of “IS” received

        the DM should work out the amount of the overpayment1. The Secretary of State
        can recover the amount overpaid where the conditions in DMG 09004 are satisfied2.
                                                                 1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(11); 2 reg 36(12)


        Example

        Jackie is 70. She was in receipt of IS immediately before 6.10.03. She is treated as
        having made a claim for SPC but the DM was unable to decide that claim. Jackie
        continues to receive payments of “IS”. On 20.10.03 the DM decides that Jackie is
        entitled to SPC but at a lower amount than the “IS” she received. This is because
        the amount of benefit Jackie received was incorrect. The SPC is offset and the DM
        also considers whether the additional “IS” Jackie has received should be recovered.

        80016 - 80020

        Transferee’s assessed income period

80021   The decision of the DM on the duration of a transferee’s AIP1 takes effect on
        6.10.032 (see DMG 80071 et seq).
                                                              1 SPC Act 02, s 9; 2 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(5)

        Payment of SPC

80022   SPC is normally paid in advance1. However, SPC may be payable in arrears if the
        IS to which the transferee was entitled before 6.10.03 was paid in arrears2.
                                                   1 SS (C&P) Regs, reg 26B(4); 2 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(6)


        Example

        Before 6.10.03 Felix received IS which was combined with IB and was paid in
        arrears on a Wednesday. Felix is entitled to SPC. The DM decides that Felix’s SPC
        is paid in arrears on a Wednesday.

        Note: When Felix reaches age 65 his RP would be payable from the Monday on or
        after his birthday. From that Monday the DM would decide to pay Felix’s SPC in
        advance under the normal rules (see DMG 79106 for guidance on part-week
        payments).

80023   Except where DMG 80024 applies, where SPC is paid in arrears any decision which

        1.    supersedes1 a decision awarding SPC to a transferee and

        2.    is made on the ground that there has been a relevant change of
              circumstances since the decision was made or that it is anticipated that a
              relevant change of circumstances will occur

        will take effect from the first day of the benefit week in which the change occurs or is
        expected to occur2.
                                                               1 SS Act 98, s 10; 2 SPC Cql Regs, reg 36(7)




80024   The exception to the guidance at DMG 80023 is where the change of circumstances
        is that the transferee becomes a patient again in the same benefit week in which he
        ceased to be a patient. If this is the case, the supersession decision in respect of
        the transferee becoming a patient again takes effect from the first day of the benefit
        week following the benefit week in which the change occurs1.

        Note: See DMG 78703 for the definition of “patient”.
                                                                                              1 reg 36(7A)


80025   For the purposes of DMG 80023, benefit week means the period of seven days
        ending on the day on which SPC is payable1.
                                                                                               1 reg 36(8)


        Example

        Callum is in receipt of SPC which is paid in arrears. His benefit week runs from
        Tuesday to Monday. On Friday 14.11.03 Callum reports that his wife has started
        work and has received a payment of wages on that day. The DM supersedes the
        decision awarding SPC to Callum with effect from Tuesday 11.11.03.


        SPC paid by direct payment

80026   Where

        1.    the transferee had an award of IS payable by direct payment in accordance
              with prescribed legislation1 immediately before 6.10.03 (see DMG 08033) and

        2.    SPC is payable or treated as payable from 6.10.03

        SPC will continue to be paid by direct payment into the same bank or other account
        as the payment of IS. Any application made or treated as made and any consent
        given or treated as given in relation to the payment of IS will be treated as made or
        given in relation to the payment of SPC2.

        Claims for SPC
        Claimant not in receipt of IS before 6.10.03

80051   A claim for SPC may be made before 6.10.03 by a claimant who is not in receipt of
        IS when the claim is made1.
                                                                             1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 38(1)


80052   Where DMG 80051 applies and the DM is satisfied that, unless there is a change in
        the claimant’s circumstances before 6.10.03, the claimant will satisfy the conditions
        of entitlement to SPC the DM may

        1.    treat the claim as if made for a period beginning on 6.10.03 and

        2.    award SPC1.
                                                                                             1 reg 38(2)


80053   Where DMG 80052 2. applies, the award of SPC is subject to the condition that the
        claimant does actually satisfy the conditions of entitlement to SPC when it becomes
        payable under the award1.
                                                                                          1 reg 38(2)(b)

        Revision

80054   Where DMG 80052 2. applies, the DM may revise1 the award of SPC if the
        conditions of entitlement to SPC are found not to have been satisfied on 6.10.032.
                                                              1 SS Act 98 s 9; 2 SPC Cql Regs, reg 38(3)



        Claimant entitled to IS prior to 6.10.03 but not entitled to
        IS on 5.10.03

80055   A claimant who

        1.    on 6.10.03 has reached the qualifying age of 60 (see DMG 77032) and

        2.    was entitled to IS within the period of 6 months prior to 6.10.03 and

        3.    was not entitled to IS on 5.10.03

        should be treated as having made a claim for SPC for a period beginning on
        6.10.031.
                                                                                             1 reg 38(8)

        Example

        Rabina is aged 65. She is entitled to IS. On 26.8.03 she reports that she has
        received an inheritance of £15,000. The DM supersedes the decision awarding IS
        and decides that Rabina is not entitled to IS. Rabina does not make a further claim
        for IS before 6.10.03. She is treated as having made a claim for SPC for a period
        beginning on 6.10.03.

        80056 - 80060


        Claim for IS made before 6.10.03 treated as a claim for
        SPC

80061   Where a claim for IS is made in the period of six months before 6.10.03 the DM may
        treat it also as a claim for SPC if the claimant

        1.    is not entitled to IS and

        2.    is aged 60 or over when the claim is made or will be age 60 on 6.10.031.
                                                                          1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 38(6)


        Example

        Jason makes a claim for IS on 7.7.03. The DM decides that Jason is not entitled to
        IS because he has capital of £12,500. Jason is age 60 on 1.9.03. The DM also
        treats Jason’s claim for IS as a claim for SPC.

80062   Where DMG 80061 applies and the DM is satisfied that, unless there is a change in
        the claimant’s circumstances before 6.10.03, the claimant will satisfy the conditions
        of entitlement to SPC the DM may

        1.    treat the claim as if made for a period beginning on 6.10.03 and

        2.    award SPC

        An award of SPC will be subject to the condition that the claimant does actually
        satisfy the conditions of entitlement to SPC when it becomes payable under the
        award. The DM may revise the award of SPC if the conditions of entitlement to SPC
                                                           1
        are found not to have been satisfied on 6.10.03 .
                                                                                        1 reg 38(7)
        Claim for SPC treated as a claim for IS

80063   The DM may treat a claim for SPC made before 6.10.03 by a claimant who is not in
        receipt of IS as also a claim for IS made on the same day1.
                                                                                        1 reg 38(9)




Vol 13 Amendment 14 October 2007
        Example

        Dawn makes a claim for SPC on 16.6.03. The DM decides that Dawn is entitled to
        SPC from 6.10.03. The DM also considers the conditions of entitlement to IS. Dawn
        satisfies those conditions so the DM decides she would have been entitled to IS if
        she had claimed it. The DM therefore treats Dawn’s claim for SPC as a claim for IS
        made on 16.6.03.


        Claim for SPC made on or after 6.10.03

        Claim treated as made on 6.10.03

80064   Where a claim for SPC is made in the period of 12 months beginning on 6.10.03 the
        DM may treat it as made on 6.10.03 if the claimant satisfied the conditions of
        entitlement to SPC on that day1.
                                                                           1 SPC Cql Regs, reg 38(4)


        Example

        Lisa makes a claim for SPC on 5.5.04. She satisfied the conditions for entitlement to
        SPC on 6.10.03. The DM treats Lisa’s claim for SPC as having been made on
        6.10.03.


        Claim treated as made on a day other than 6.10.03

80065   Where

        1.    DMG 80064 does not apply only because the claimant does not satisfy the
              conditions of entitlement to SPC on 6.10.03 and

        2.    the claimant does satisfy those conditions on a day after 6.10.03 but before
              the date on which the claim is received by the Secretary of State

        the DM should treat the claim as having been made on the day the conditions of
        entitlement to SPC were first satisfied1.
                                                                                         1 reg 38(5)


        Example

        Christopher also makes a claim for SPC on 5.5.04. He was born on 2.2.44. He did
        not satisfy the conditions of entitlement to SPC on 6.10.03 but did satisfy the
        conditions of entitlement on 2.2.04. The DM treats Christopher’s claim for SPC as
        having been made on 2.2.04.

        When transitional provisions apply

80071   The following guidance explains the effects of the transitional provisions on the AIP.
        See DMG Chapter 83 for full guidance on the AIP. Transitional provisions apply to
        the first AIP specified in respect of a person who

        1.    reaches or has reached the age of 65 or whose partner reaches or has
              reached that age on or before 6.10.03 and

        2.    is awarded SPC with effect from 6.10.031.
                                                                          1 SPC Cql Regs, regs 37(2)


80072   Where DMG 80071 applies the DM should set an AIP of at least five years but not
        exceeding seven years beginning on 6.10.031.
                                                                                         1 reg 37(1)


        Note: The period of the first AIP allotted to a transferee will be decided by reference
        to their national insurance number.
