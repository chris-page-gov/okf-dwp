    INTRODUCTION

1   This memo gives guidance on the application of The Universal Credit (Claims and
    Payments)(Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2017 (S.S.I 2017 No. 436) which come
                              1
    into force on 31.01.18.

                      1 The Universal Credit (Claims and Payments)(Scotland) Amendment Regulations 2017, reg 1




    WHO THIS GUIDANCE APPLIES TO

2   ADM memo 26/17 gave guidance on twice-monthly payments of UC and direct
    payments for rent and services for a person applying for or awarded UC who resides
    in Scotland (and any one of the members of a couple living in Scotland who jointly
    have been awarded UC). This memo amends the guidance given in ADM memo
    26/17.
