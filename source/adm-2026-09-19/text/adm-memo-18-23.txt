Introduction

1. [See ADM memo 2-26] This memo gives guidance on The Carer’s Assistance (Carer Support Payment)

   (Scotland) Regulations 2023 (Consequential Amendments) Order 20231 which came into force on
   19.11.23.


                                                                                         1 SI 2023 No 1214



Background

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect the introduction of the Carer
   Support Payment (CSP) which replaces CA in Scotland.


   Note: This guidance is applicable to UC, JSA and ESA.


                                                                   1 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23



Social Fund Funeral Payment

3. ADM L3408 and Appendix 1 to ADM Chapter L3 gives guidance on arrears of benefit payable to the
   deceased not being counted as assets of the deceased when considering deductions from the
   amount of award of funeral payment. From 19.11.23 arrears of CSP payable to the deceased as at the

   date of death are excluded from the assets of the deceased 1.


                                                                       1 SFMFE (Gen) Regs, reg 10(1A)(ca)



Universal Credit

  The carer element



4. ADM F6033 gives guidance that when considering whether a carer element is payable, where two or
more people are regularly and substantially caring for the same severely disabled person, only one of

them will be entitled to have the carer element included in the UC award. From 19.11.23 regulations 1
provide that where a




   1. person (A) has an entitlement to CSP and




   2. person (B) has an entitlement to the carer element of UC
they must jointly elect who shall have entitlement to the carer element. If there is no such election, the
Secretary of State, in consultation with the Scottish Ministers, shall decide.




                                               1 UC regs, reg 29(5)(6); CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23




  Regular and substantial caring responsibilities



5. ADM F6010 gives guidance that to qualify for an award of a carer element, the claimant must have
regular and substantial caring responsibilities for a severely disabled person and would meet that

condition if they satisfy the conditions for entitlement to a CA 1. From 19.11.23 regulations2 adds to the
list




   1. those who would be entitled to a CA but for the fact that




         1.1 their earnings exceed the prescribed limits or




         1.2 they are resident or treated as resident in Scotland or


         1.3 they have a genuine and sufficient link to Scotland and




       2. those entitled to CSP.




                                                1 UC Regs, reg 30(1); 2 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23




  Entitlement to a housing cost element – renters exempt from HCC

6. ADM F3151 gives guidance on considering whether a renter is exempt from a HCC deduction. From
19.11.23 CSP is added to the list of relevant benefits the renter may be in receipt of to be exempt 1.




                                                                                 1 UC Regs, Sch 4, para 16(2)


  Unearned income

7. CSP is taken into account as unearned income up to a maximum of the amount a claimant would

   receive if they had an entitlement to CA1.


                                                                                  1 UC Regs, reg 66(1)(b)(iiia)




Jobseeker’s allowance

 Waiting days

8. ADM R2702 gives guidance on when a JSA claimant does not have to serve waiting days. From

   19.11.23 CSP is added to the list of qualifying benefits 1.


                                                                                     1 JSA Regs 13, reg 36(1)


  Linked periods

9. ADM R1027 gives guidance on linked periods. From 19.11.23 a linked period will include when a

   claimant is entitled to a CSP1.




                                                                 1 JSA Regs 13, reg 39(2); SS CB Act 92, s 70




 Carers and remunerative work

10. For the purpose of whether a JSA claimant is in remunerative work where they are a carer 1 (see

R2252), CSP2 is added to the list of qualifying benefits from 19.11.23. See Chapter R2 (JSA conditions of
entitlement) for further details.


                                     1 JSA Regs 13, reg 42(3)(c)(iii); 2 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23
Employment and Support Allowance




 First contribution condition

11. U1085 gives guidance on relaxation of the first contribution condition 1 where specified conditions
are met, including where the ESA claimant is entitled to CA in the last complete tax year immediately

before the relevant benefit year in which the first day of LCW occurred 2. From 19.11.23 this includes

where the claimant is entitled to CSP3.


                                          1 WR Act 07, Sch 1, Part 1, para 1(4)(b); 2 ESA Regs 13, reg 9(2)(a);


                                                                     3 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23




  Waiting days

12. U1303 gives guidance on when an ESA claimant does not have to serve waiting days, including where

entitlement to ESA begins within 12 weeks of the end of entitlement to specified benefits 1. From

19.11.23, CSP2 is added to the list of qualifying benefits.


                                       1 ESA Regs 13, reg 85(2)(a); 2 CA (CSP)(Scot) Regs 23 (CA) Order 23




Universal Credit (Transitional Protections)

  Amount of transitional SDP element: joint claimants

13. Memo ADM 13/23 paragraphs 6 – 10 gives guidance on the amount of the transitional SDP element
payable to joint claimants in the first AP where a person has become a carer for either of them in that

AP1. From 19.11.23 the definition of carer includes a person who is entitled to and in receipt of CSP 2.


Note: See M6710 et seq for detailed guidance on the transitional SDP element.
