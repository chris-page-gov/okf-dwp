Winter Fuel Payments
L5000 This guidance applies from 15.09.25 in respect of Winter Fuel Payment claims from Winter 2025
onwards.


L5001 A WFP is a one-off annual payment based on an individual’s circumstances during a specified
Qualifying Week. It is payable to those who in or before the QW have reached pensionable age. Winter

Fuel Payments will be automatically issued using information from Departmental records 1. If a payment
is not paid automatically, the person can make a claim for it before 31 March in the following year.


Note: The provisions for payments in the EEA and Switzerland expired on 1 April 2025. From Winter
2025/26, WFP will no longer be payable in the EEA and Switzerland.


                                                                                      1 SFWFP Regs, reg 5


L5002 The QW in any year is the week beginning on the third Monday in September of that year 1.


                                                                                    1 SFWFP Regs, reg 1(3)

Persons not entitled to a Winter Fuel Payment

L5011 Persons will not qualify for a WFP if throughout the QW they are


1. a partner of and living with a person who has reached pensionable age in or before the QW and who

has been, or falls to be, paid a relevant benefit in respect of the QW 1 or


2. receiving free in-patient treatment and have done so for 52 weeks or more ending immediately before

the QW2 or


3. detained in custody under a sentence imposed by a court 3 or


4. in residential care and the period of at least 12 weeks ending immediately before the qualifying week
(disregarding any period of temporary absence) and a relevant benefit has been, or falls to be, paid in

respect of any day falling within the QW4 or


5. having been ordinarily resident in England or Wales on a day within the QW, becomes ordinarily

resident in Scotland or Northern Ireland by the last day of that week 5 or


6. has not made a claim for a WFP on or before 31 March following the qualifying week in respect of the

winter following that week where it is required to make one and payment is not made automatically 6

Amount of Winter Fuel Payment

L5023 Where a person is entitled to a WFP, the amount payable depends on their age, living
arrangements, and whether a relevant benefit is payable


1. £3001 is payable if the person has reached the age of 80 on or before the QW; or


2. £3002 is payable if the person has not reached the age of 80 on or before the QW and a relevant


  benefit has been or falls to be paid to them in or before the QW and in that week


    1. they are living with and the partner of another person who has reached the age of 80 in or


    before that week.


3. £2003 is payable if the person has not reached the age of 80 in or before the QW or
4. £2004 is payable if the person has reached the age of 80 in or before the QW; and a relevant


 benefit has not been, and does not fall to be, paid to them in or before the QW; and in that week


    1. they are living with a person entitled to a WFP who has not reached the age of 80.


5. £1505 is payable if the person has reached the age of 80 in or before the QW; and a relevant benefit
has not been, and does not fall to be, paid to them in or before the QW; and in that week


    1. they are living with another person entitled to a WFP who has reached the age of 80 or


     2. in residential care.


6. £1006 is payable if the person has not reached the age of 80 on or before the QW; and a relevant
benefit


  has not been, and does not fall to be, paid to them in or before the QW; and in that week


     1. they are living with a person entitled to a WFP or


     2. in residential care.


Note 1: Paragraph 1 is subject to paragraph 4 and 5.


Note 2: Paragraph 2 is subject to paragraph 3 and 6.


Note 3: Paragraph 3 is subject to paragraph 6.


Note 4: See Appendix 1 for illustrative table of amounts.


              1 SFWFP Regs, reg 3(4)(link is external); 2 reg 3(3); 3 reg 3(1); 4 reg 3(5) 5 reg 3(6) 6 reg 3(2)


Example 1


Ben is 78 and lives with his wife, Carol, who is also eligible for a WFP. Neither Ben nor Carol receives a
relevant benefit during the qualifying week. Ben is entitled to £100 WFP. Carol would also be entitled
to £100, assuming she is also aged under 80.


Example 2


Irene is 85 and lives in a care home. She does not receive any relevant benefits during the qualifying
week. Irene is entitled to a Winter Fuel Payment of £150.


Example 3


Dorothy is 82 and lives in a shared household with her sister, Margaret, who is 85 and eligible for a WFP.
Neither receives a relevant benefit. Dorothy is entitled to £150 WFP. Margaret would also be entitled
to £150.

Example 1


Bill and Ethel are a married couple both aged over 80. Bill receives SPC for himself and his wife so he is
entitled to a WFP of £300 and Ethel will not be entitled to a WFP.


Example 2


Pepé, Freddie and Roxie are members of a polygamous household. Freddie who is aged 70 is
automatically paid a WFP of £200. Although Pepé and Roxie have both reached the qualifying age for
SPC they will not be entitled to a WFP as Freddie has already been paid a WFP.


Example 3


Donald reaches pensionable age on 16.09.24 and is living in the Republic of Ireland and in receipt of a
benefit equivalent to SPC so is in the category referred to in ADM L5002 who have to make a claim for
WFP. On the 1.4.25 he makes a claim for a WFP. As Donald has missed the 31.3.25 deadline he will not be
entitled to a WFP for the winter following that QW.
