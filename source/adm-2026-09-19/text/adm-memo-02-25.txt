INTRODUCTION

1. The purpose of this memo is to give guidance to decision makers (DM) on The Social Security
(Scotland) Act 2018 (Disability Assistance) (Consequential Amendments) Order 2024 1. The amendments
came into force on 21.10.24.


                                           1 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024; SI 2024 No 919



BACKGROUND

2. This order1 makes changes to social security legislation to reflect the introduction by the Scottish
Government (SG) of the Pension Age Disability Payment (PADP). This is to replace Attendance
Allowance (AA) for pension age adult’s resident in Scotland.


                                                               1 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024



UNIVERSAL CREDIT

Claimant disabled and over State Pension Credit Age
3. G3030 provides guidance on when a claimant is treated as having LCWRA where they have reached

the qualifying age for State Pension Credit1. From 21.10.23 Entitlement to PADP is included in the list of
qualifying benefits.


                                                                                     1 UC Regs, Sch 9, para 5


When the benefit cap does not apply
4. E5042 provides guidance on when the BC does not apply when (a) a person is in receipt of a specified
benefit, or (b) the person, or child or qualifying young person for whom they are responsible, is entitled to

a specified benefit but it is not being received because of residence in a care home or hospital 1. From
21.10.24 PADP is included in the list of specified benefits.


                                                                                    1 UC Reg 83(1)(ca) and (h)

5. F2182 et seq provides guidance on the occupation condition when a claimant is required to move

at F2183.

NEW STYLE JOBSEEKERS ALLOWANCE

Carers and remunerative work
9. Recipients of NSJSA are not entitled to an award if they are engaged in a remunerative work. However,

a claimant who is a carer1 (see R2252) for a recipient of a qualifying benefit, including PADP (added to the
list of qualifying benefits with effect from 21.10.24), shall be exempt from this provision. See Chapter R2
(JSA conditions of entitlement) for further details.


                             1 JSA Regs 13, reg 42(3)(c)(iii); 2 SS (Scot) Act 2018 (Dis Ass) (CA) Order 2024



NEW STYLE EMPLOYMENT AND SUPPORT ALLOWANCE

Definition of “person in Hardship”
10. A period of disqualification from receiving ESA due to misconduct does not apply if the claimant
qualifies as a “person in hardship.” U6038 provides guidance on the circumstances under which a
claimant or their partner may be considered a person in hardship, specifically if they have been awarded
a specified benefit.


From 21.10.24, PADP will be added to the list of specified benefits 1.


                                                                                   1 ESA Regs 13, reg 94(3)

SOCIAL FUND FUNERAL PAYMENTS

Arrears of benefit paid on or after date of death
15. L3408 gives guidance on arrears of benefit payable to the deceased not being counted as assets of
the deceased when considering deductions from the amount of award of funeral payment. From
21.10.24 arrears of PADP payable to the deceased as at the date of death are excluded from the assets

of the deceased1 and included in the list of benefits excluded as assets of the deceased in Appendix 1 to
ADM Chapter L3.
