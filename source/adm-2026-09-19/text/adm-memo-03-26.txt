introduction

1. This memo gives guidance on the Universal Credit (Removal of Two Child Limit) Act 2026, which

  comes into force on 6.4.26.1

                                                               1. UC (Removal of Two Child Limit) Act s1




Background

2. Since 6 April 2017 the child element (CE) of Universal Credit (UC) has been awarded only in respect
  of the first and second child or QYP for whom the claimant is responsible, unless an exception

  applies. (the two-child limit)1

                                                                               1. UC Regs, reg 24A and Sch 12




Changes

3. From 6.4.26 that limit is removed and a CE will be payable in respect of any child or QYP the claimant
  is responsible for. (ADM F1056) The ordering of children and QYPs, and whether any exceptions
  apply, no longer needs to be considered.

4. An amount in respect of any child or QYP for whom the claimant is responsible will be included in their

  award of UC in the AP commencing on or after the 6.4.261.
                                                                  UC(Removal of 2 child limit) Act, s1(4).

6. However, the two-child limit must still be applied when considering entitlement to a CE for any period
  prior to 6.4.26.
