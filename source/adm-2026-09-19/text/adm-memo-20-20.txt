    INTRODUCTION

1   The Secretary of State for Health and Social Care has announced a scheme of
    payments for people on low incomes in areas with a high incidence of Coronavirus,
    who need to self-isolate and are unable to work from home. Applications, payments
    and disputes are the responsibility of Local Authorities. The scheme started on 1.9.20.


    ELIGIBILITY

2   Eligibility is restricted to people who are resident in Blackburn with Darwen, Oldham or
    Pendle and who meet the following criteria:

    1.     They have received notification from NHS Test and Trace;

    2.     They have agreed to comply with the notification from NHS Test and Trace and
           provided contact details to their LA;

    3.     They are employed or self-employed;

    4.     They are unable to work from home and will lose income as a result.

    5.     They are currently receiving UC, WTC, income-related ESA, income-based
           JSA, IS or SPC or HB.

    Virus infection rates are monitored and the scope of the scheme may be subject to
    change in the future.
    THE TREATMENT OF PAYMENTS FOR UC PURPOSES

3   Payments under this scheme are not included in unearned income to be taken into
    account1 and therefore do not affect UC.
