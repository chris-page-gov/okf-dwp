INTRODUCTION
1. This Memo follows on from ADM Memo 12/25 which gave guidance on The Welfare Reform Act 2012
(Commencement No. 35) (Abolition of Benefits) Order 2025 1. That Commencement Order is now
amended through a new Order – The Welfare Reform Act 2012 (Commencement No.35) (Abolition of
Benefits) (Amendment) Order 20262. This Memo gives guidance on the new Order and should be read
in conjunction with ADM Memo 12/25.
                                                           1 WRA 2012, CO 35; 2 WRA 2012, CO35 (Amndt)

2. Commencement Order No.35 is amended in relation to Income-related Employment and Support
Allowance (ESA(IR)) and Housing Benefit (HB) for working age persons.



ABOLITION OF ESA (IR)
3. For anyone whose ESA(IR) award is not already abolished the date of abolition will be 01/07/26,
except for
       3.1 those claimants who have a two-week run-on period of ESA(IR) still to elapse, for whom the
       abolition will take place at the end of the run-on period1 or
       3.2 where an appointee is acting for the claimant immediately before 01/07/26, or where the
       Secretary of State determines an appointee was likely to be needed (in the six months prior to
       01/07/26), where there is no abolition date2 until one of the events in paragraph 4 occurs.
4. Where someone in the 3.2 cohort does make a claim to UC, or does not make a claim to UC by the
deadline set in their Managed Migration notice, the normal abolition dates then apply3 (i.e. where a claim
was made, after the two-week run-on period4, or where no claim was made, after two weeks beginning
with the deadline date).5

ABOLITION OF HB
LEAVING TEMPORARY OR SPECIFIED ACCOMODATION
5. The original Commencement Order No.35 provided for the following abolition date for HB claimants in
temporary or specified accommodation, who then leave that accommodation 1 (see paragraph 13 of ADM
Memo 12/25):
       13. The date of abolition of HB in relation to an award of HB where the claimant
               13.1 is not entitled to UC, IS JSA(IB) or ESA(IR), and

               13.2 is not someone over the qualifying age for state pension credit (including those in
               protected mixed age couples,) and
               13.3 is entitled to that award in respect of specified or temporary accommodation,

       is any day falling on or after 14/11/25 that is the day after the last day on which the claimant is
       entitled to HB in respect of specified or temporary accommodation.

6. This now applies for the period from 14/11/25 up to (but not including) 01/07/262. On 01/07/26 they
will be subject to the provisions set out in paragraph 7 below when leaving temporary or specified
accommodation.

NOT IN TEMPORARY OR SPECIFIED ACCOMODATION

7. The date for the abolition of HB for persons not in temporary or specified accommodation1 is
01/07/26, except where the award of HB

       7.1. is already terminated2,

       7.2 is due to terminate at the end of a two-week run-on period 3 (the abolition date being the day
       after the run-on period)3
       7.3 is made to a claimant also in receipt of old-style ESA, who had an appointee appointed
       immediately before 01/07/26 or needs to have one appointed (see paragraph 3.2. and 4 above)4,

       7.4. is for someone in prison (or serving a sentence in hospital) 5 immediately before 01/07/266
       (the abolition date being the day after the day they are released from prison/hospital), or
       7.5 is for someone over the qualifying age for state pension credit, including those in protected
       mixed age couples (for whom there is no abolition date).7
