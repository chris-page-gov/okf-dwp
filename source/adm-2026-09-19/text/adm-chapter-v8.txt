Introduction V8001 - V8014

Introduction
V8001 This Chapter gives guidance on


1. when the provisions for claiming new style ESA apply (see V8020 et seq) and


2. reassessment of IB and SDA for new style ESA (see V8120 et seq) and


3. transition from old style ESA (see V8125 et seq) and


4 transition from new style ESA (see V8160 et seq) and


5. sanctions (see V8200 et seq).


V8002 The conditions for claiming new style ESA depend on whether


1. the claimant lives in a digital service (formerly known as Full Service) area and


2. any restrictions on claiming UC apply.


Note: From 25.7.22, all restrictions on claims for UC have been removed.


V8003 From 29.4.13, claims for UC could be made by people who satisfied specified conditions and lived
in selected areas (the relevant districts). From 16.6.14 the specified conditions became the gateway
conditions, and the relevant districts were known as the Live Service area.


V8004 Starting from 27.1.16, a phased conversion of postcode districts or part-districts in the Live
Service area began, converting them to digital service areas referred to as the designated postcodes
where the gateway conditions no longer apply. Since 12.12.18, all GB postcode districts and part-districts
have been converted to digital service areas.


Note 1: From 25.7.22, all restrictions on claims for UC have been removed.


Note 2: See Appendix 1 to ADM Chapter M5 (Claims for UC – Digital service area) for details of the
designated postcodes included in the digital service area.

Introduction

V8020 V8021 et seq gives guidance on when the provisions for claiming new style ESA apply. However,
the new style ESA provisions apply only for as long as the award continues. On any subsequent claim,
DMs will have to consider again whether the new style or old style provisions apply. Also, DMs should
note that the new style ESA provisions may no longer apply if the claimant’s circumstances change (see
V8100 et seq).


Note: Since 12.12.18, the new style ESA provisions apply to all claims made in GB, and on or after 1.2.19 to
claims from people living outside GB, unless any restriction on claiming UC applies. From 25.7.22 all
restrictions on claims for UC have been removed. See Chapter M5 (Claims for UC – Digital Service area)
for further details.

Introduction

V8120 From 11.10.10, the Secretary of State began to make decisions on whether the awards of
claimants who, on or after that date, have an existing award of IB, SDA or IS, qualify for conversion to
ESA. The process of conversion is known as IB reassessment. V8125 et seq gives guidance on the effect
of reassessment of IB and SDA on new style ESA.


Note: See DMG Chapter 45 for detailed guidance on IB reassessment.


V8121 Where V8123 applies, the normal rules for conversion are modified 1 to


1. remove all references to ESA(IR) and IS and


2. replace all references to old style ESA rules with references to new style ESA 2.


Note: Where an award of UC is made to a claimant entitled to IS on the grounds of disability, the award of

IS is terminated, and the claimant is referred for the WCA under UC rules 3. See ADM Chapter M6
(Effects of transition to UC – Digital Service area) for further details.
