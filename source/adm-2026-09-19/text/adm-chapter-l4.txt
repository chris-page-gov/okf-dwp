L4000 A Social Fund Cold Weather Payment (CWP) involves a payment of £25 1 for each cold weather
period experienced by qualifying persons, in order to help provide for heating expenses. It is normally
paid automatically by the relevant computer system.

Making a claim
L4002 If a CWP has not been paid automatically or clerically by the relevant computer systems, the DM
should treat any representations about entitlement as a claim and decide whether there is any

entitlement1. The claimant has a right of appeal against the DM’s decision to the First-tier Tribunal 2.


                                                       1 SS Act 98, s 8(1)(a); 2 s12(1)(a), SS CS (D&A) Regs 26(b)


Time limits for claim
L4003 A CWP cannot be made more than 26 weeks from and including the last day of the ‘winter

period’ (see L4004)in which the relevant period of cold weather falls1. This allows time for a claimant to
report a change if they feel they may have qualified after receiving their winter fuel bills in association
with the winter season for which help with heating expenses is provided.


                                                                                    1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(6)


Winter Period
L4004 Winter period is defined as 1st November to 31st March of the following year.1
                                                                              1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 1(2)


Example


The claimant has been in receipt of IS since September 2010. In April 2020 he applies for a backdated
payment of a CWP stating his child was born on 2.1.20 (but he did not notify the Department of the birth
at the time). A cold weather period is recorded at the authorised Met office site for the claimant’s
postcode for the period 10.1.20 to 16.1.20. The DM decides he is entitled to a CWP as the claimant meets
the entitlement criteria -there was a cold weather period recorded for the claimant’s postcode, he was
entitled to IS and had a child in his family younger than 5 - and the claim was made within the time limits.


Cold Weather Period
L4005 A cold weather period is a period of seven consecutive days during which the average mean daily

temperature is recorded as, or is forecast to be, zero degrees celsius or below 1, by the designated Met
Office site (see L4011).


                                                                              1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 1(2)


L4006 Any day included in one cold weather period cannot be included in any other cold weather period.


Overlaps of recorded and forecast periods of cold weather
L4007 When a forecast period shares one or more days with a recorded period, the shared days will

form an overlap period1.


                                                                              1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 1(2)


L4008 If both a recorded and forecast period of cold weather are linked by an overlap period, the DM

should only make payment for the forecast period1.


                                                                             1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(3)


Example




Eligible period is days 1-13 inclusive. A CWP is made for the period 7-13.




L4009 Payment may be made for the last recorded period of cold weather 1 if
       1. there is a continuous period of recorded and forecast periods and


       2. each of those periods is linked by an overlap period and


       3. the total number of recorded periods exceeds the total number of forecast periods.


                                                                               1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(4)


Example




1. Eligible period 15 days 1-14 inclusive


2. A CWP is made for the forecast period 5-11


3. A CWP is made for the last recorded period 8-14.

       1. would have been entitled to a CWP but for the fact that a forecast period takes priority and


       2. is unable to satisfy the qualifying conditions for at least one day of the forecast period 1.

The designated Met Office site
L4011 PO postcode districts1 are linked to authorized weather sites. PO postcodes have four parts to
them


1. area example LS14 1NX


2. district example LS14 1NX


3. sector example LS14 1NX


4. group of houses/building example LS14 1NX.


A postcode district may also include a letter at the end example SW1A.


The DM should ignore the letter at the end of the postcode district when deciding which weather site
covers the claimant's home.


Note: When a forecast or recorded period of cold weather occurs the Met Office notify SF Problem
Management, Live Support, who then notify DOs.


Note: From 9.7.20 all designated Met Office recording ‘stations’ are now referred to as ‘sites’. 1


                                                                  1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg. 1(2); reg. reg 2(1)


Primary, Secondary and Alternative Sites
L4012 Primary site1 means a site designated by the Secretary of State as a primary site for a postcode

district2.


Note: A list of the primary sites can be found at the end of this chapter.


                                                                   1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(7); 2 reg 2A(1)


L4013 Secondary site1 means a site designated by the Secretary of State as a secondary site for a

postcode district2. A secondary site would be used in circumstances where


        1. a primary site was unable to provide temperature information in respect of a particular day or


        2. the Met Office is unable to produce a forecast in respect of a particular period at a primary site.


                                                                  1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(7); 2 reg 2A(2)
L4014 Alternative site1 means a site which


       1. in the opinion of the Met Office, is the most geographically and climatologically representative
       for the postcode district in which the home of the claimant is situated and


       2. is able to provide the temperature information for the relevant


       recorded or forecasted period of cold weather.


Note: An alternative site would be used to determine whether there is a recorded or forecasted period of
cold weather in circumstances where


       1. the primary and secondary site is unable produce the requisite information or


       2. the primary site is unable to produce the requisite information and there is no secondary site
       specified.


                                                                             1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(7)


L4015 Where a primary site is unable to provide temperature information in respect of a particular day,
the mean daily temperature of that day at


       1. the secondary site or


       2. an alternative site, where


               2.1 there is no secondary site designated or


               2.2 the secondary site is unable to provide temperature information in respect of that day


is to be used to determine whether or not there is a recorded period of cold weather at a primary site1.


                                                                            1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(1A)


L4016 Where the Met Office is unable to produce a forecast in respect of a particular day at a primary
site, the forecast in respect of that period produced at


       1. the secondary site or


       2. the alternative site, where


               2.1 there is no secondary site designated or


               2.2 the secondary site is unable to produce a forecast in respect of that period


is to be used to determine whether or not there is a forecasted period of cold weather at the primary
site1.


                                                                               1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(1B)


L4017 Where the home of the claimant is not situated within a postcode district for which a primary site
is designated, the home is to be treated as if it were situated within a postcode district


         1. which, in the opinion of the Met Office, is the most geographically and climatologically
         representative of that postcode district and


         2. for which a primary site is designated1.




                                                                                 1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2(2)


Note: A list of all secondary and alternative sites is located at the end of this chapter.


Publication and Review of Sites
L4018 A primary site must be designated for each postcode district. A secondary site may be

designated for each postcode district1.


                                                                        1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2A(1) and (2)


L4019 Details of designated primary (and where appropriate, secondary) sites must be published. They
are found at the end of this chapter. A CWP Postcode Tool is available at
https://coldweatherpayments.dwp.gov.uk/


L4020 Each designation must be reviewed every 12 months, in the period beginning 1 st November and

ending 31st October1.


                                                                               1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2C(2)


L4021 If, on review or at any other time, a designation is no longer appropriate, the Secretary of State
must


         1. vary the designation expediently and


         2. publish details of the varied designation1, in an appropriate manner2.


Note: When determining whether to vary a designation, regard must be given to any recommendation

made by the Met Office3.


                                                       1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2C(3); 2 reg 2C(4); 3 reg 2C(5)
L4022 For the purposes of a review or a variation1, whether a designation is appropriate includes in
particular, whether the site designated


         1. is geographically and climatologically representative for the relevant postcode district and


         2. provides accurate temperature information.


                                                                             1 SFCWP (Gen) Regs, reg 2C(6)


Definitions
Care Home


L4023 Care home means1 an establishment which provides accommodation, together with nursing or
personal care for people who


1. are or have been ill


2. have or who have had a mental disorder


3. are disabled or infirm


4. are or have been dependent on alcohol or drugs


other than a hospital, independent clinic, children’s home or other establishment excepted by
regulations.

Employment


L4024 Means employment under a contract of service, or in an office, including an elective office.


Gainful Self-employment


L4025 A person is in gainful self employment where


1.They are carrying on a trade, profession or vocation as their main employment;


2. Their earnings from that trade, profession or vocation are treated as self-employed earnings for the
purpose of regulations made under section 8(3) of the 2012 Act; and


3. The trade, profession or vocation is organised, developed, regular and carried on in expectation of
profit
