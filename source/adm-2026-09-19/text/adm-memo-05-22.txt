INTRODUCTION


    1. This memo gives guidance on The UC (Work-Related Requirements) In Work Pilot Scheme
       (Extension) Order 20221. This order comes into force on 19.2.22 and extends to England, Wales

       and Scotland.

       with 19.2.22.
