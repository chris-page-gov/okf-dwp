introduction

1. This memo contains details about the income tax and the Social Security contribution rates for
2026/2027 and the increase in UC benefit rates from 06.4.26. The rates are introduced by the Social
Security Benefits Uprating Order 2026 (SI 2026/148) and the Social Security (Contributions) (Rates,
Limits and Thresholds Amendments, National Insurance Funds Payments and Extension of Veterans
Relief) Regulations 2025 (SI 2026/231) and the Social Security Benefits Uprating Regulations 2026 (SI
2026/218).This memo also contains guidance on the Employment Rights (Increase of Limits) Order 2026
(SI 2026/310) and the Social Security (Contributions)(Re-rating) Consequential Amendment Regulations
2026 (SI 2026/283) which come into effect from 06.04.26.


UPRATING


Benefit rates

2. New rates of UC will apply from the first day of the first assessment period which commences on or
after 06.04.26. DMs can find the new rates in the benefit uprating schedule and benefit desk aids and
should bear them in mind when reading examples in the ADM that contain benefit rates.


Housing costs non-dependant contribution

3. Where a non-dependant lives with the claimant, a contribution may be appropriate. The contribution is

made in the form of a deduction from the allowable housing costs1. Where applicable (see ADM F3140)
the rate of deduction is £96.55
