## Reuse for another department

Follow the [discovery-first method](methodology.md), [ontology map](ontology-use.md), [retrospective](retrospective.md) and [HMRC discovery brief](templates/hmrc-discovery-brief.md). Discover the department's native terms, sources, legislation, conventions and unresolved questions before authoring the semantic relationships. Freeze acquisition separately from interpretation, and evaluate capture, retrieval, traversal, context, provenance and answer quality as different questions.
