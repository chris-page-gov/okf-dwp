# Product and evaluation backlog

These ideas were supplied by the owner on 15 September 2026 as backlog work.
They do not change the scope or deadline of the first 4pm exemplar.

## 1. Extensive persona, journey and question evaluation

Expand the initial three personas, four user stories and eight questions into
an extensive, representative evaluation set with a pensions specialist.
Cover user goals, evidence needs, expected source routes, missing context,
negative cases, historical cases and success criteria. Evaluate retrieval,
citations, interpretation boundaries and task completion separately. The
initial authored set is a starting point, not an extensive evaluation suite.

## 2. A benefits engine

Use the information to explore a benefits engine. Before implementation,
agree its benefit scope, decision authority, legal-source hierarchy, effective
dates, rule representation, treatment of uncertainty and expert-maintained
test cases. Keep the evidence graph and executable decision rules distinct.
No such engine or entitlement calculation is part of the current exemplar.

## 3. Application and change-of-circumstances journeys

Use the information to design customer journeys for applying and reporting a
change of circumstances. Research user needs, assisted-digital routes,
accessibility, information requirements, privacy, consent, validation and the
official service/integration boundaries before building operational flows.
Initial prototypes should use explicitly synthetic cases and retain clear
unofficial status.

## Related discovery

Review the [logged tribunal decisions, calculators and CASA framework](future-sources.md)
when shaping these increments. They have not yet been assessed or imported.
