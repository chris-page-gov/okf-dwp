## Why the files are small

A catalogue lists the selected records. Choose a record to retrieve its complete text and metadata through bounded parts. The reader verifies their hashes before presenting the complete value. Relationships and diagnostics are separate streams. The complete original package is also available for a client that can accept its size.

The 225-file archive contains 2,766,291 bytes. The browser does not download every file when the page opens. The [publication declaration](../evidence-examples/registry.json) binds the approved cases, exact original inputs and exporter files. A hash match establishes delivery integrity, not current law or correct interpretation.

## Publication boundary

These fixed public questions are explicitly approved demonstration examples. The service does not retain anonymous visitors' questions. This archive is not an AI-answer audit log, a new evidence assembly or a finding that the evidence is complete. The retained public SDK check and the separate website publication check have different scopes.
