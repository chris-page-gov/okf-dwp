Reviewed on **15 September 2026**. This is a project decision based on public
catalogue and terms pages, not a legal opinion or a CPAG-approved integration.

## Addition to the bundle

The bundle includes one **external reference** to the Welfare Benefits Handbook,
published by Child Poverty Action Group. Its title, publisher links, access
boundary and project-authored relevance note are searchable in Explorer. The
welfare rights adviser persona links to it; it links to the Pension Credit topic.
These are navigation relationships, not evidence of any particular benefit rule.

CPAG's [catalogue](https://cpag.org.uk/welfare-rights/online-handbooks) marks this
handbook as a subscriber resource. The catalogue's current link names 2026/27;
the subscriber edition and contents were not inspected. Its public
[housing-costs index](https://cpag.org.uk/welfare-rights/key-topics/housing-costs)
links a Pension Credit chapter, establishing relevance without acquiring it.

**No handbook text, pages, tables or rule interpretations are included.**
The DWP source inventory remains 36 PDFs and 1,524 pages. CPAG is counted as
an external reference, never as an acquired source document or searchable
handbook corpus. The CLI continues to search only the captured DWP pages.

## Rights decision

CPAG's [terms and conditions](https://cpag.org.uk/terms-and-conditions) state:

- Website clause 6.2 reserves reproduction and republication rights.
- Website clause 11.2 directs content reuse requests through PLSclear.
- Website clause 12 restricts automated extraction and specified AI uses,
  subject to its stated legal limitations.
- Subscription clauses 9.2 and 9.3 reserve reproduction/distribution rights
  and require prior written consent for subscription content in AI tools or
  automated processes.

The project therefore adds original metadata and links only. Any later content
integration needs a documented permission covering the intended extraction,
AI processing, derived representations and public redistribution. A subscription
has not been treated as that permission. No account, user-held contract or
publisher permission has been examined, and no permission request was sent.

The [review receipt](evidence/cpag-access-review.json) records primary URLs,
clause locators and retrieval limitations. It contains project-authored findings,
not a copy of CPAG's website or handbook. The tool returned parsed public pages
without raw HTTP status; that is recorded explicitly.
