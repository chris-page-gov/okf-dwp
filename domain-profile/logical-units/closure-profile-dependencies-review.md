# Scoped dependency retention for the absence profiles

`closure-profile-dependencies.yamlld` extends two existing profiles. It changes
neither their activation concepts nor their scope or original missing
obligations. It adds nine required destinations and nine directed paths, all
independently checked against existing authored edges. The maximum path depth is
three; the configured depth-four limit is unchanged. A requirement expresses what
the evidence package should retain for inspection, not a claimant condition.

## Pension Credit claimant absence

DMG 077001 explicitly references 04642. Retain that target, its governing
04640–04641 scope, the expressly named 04643–04644 late-notification exception
and the 04655 benefit-week definition. These prevent the advance/arrears rule
from appearing without its stated qualification or the meaning of benefit week.

The other named exception group (04646–04654) remains reachable through its
existing reference edge but is not made universally required for an abroad
question. Income changes, prisoners, hospital discharge, Scottish benefits and
death need their own circumstances. The original supersession, legal-version,
applicability and specialist-review obligations remain unresolved. This does not
establish that the frozen overseas guidance includes all later amendments.

## Universal Credit temporary absence

C1988 explicitly references memo 09/25. Retain its complete temporary-absence
section, the introduction's decision-date and unchanged-entitlement statements,
benefit and territorial scopes, and the complete UC example. The example retains
absence already spent before evacuation; the other memo examples and HRT/PPT date
lists are not required merely because they were reviewed.

C1987 includes a child/qualifying-young-person alternative and directs readers to
Chapter E2. Retain the complete E2090–E2095 definition group to inspect that
alternative, including its exclusions. This does not make child responsibility
a universal UC condition. A new open branch-selection obligation explicitly
retains the missing facts for medical, death-related and emergency alternatives.
The original memo-effect, QYP, legal-version, applicability and specialist-review
obligations are preserved.

No source units, predicates, concepts, aliases, source versions or legal status
are changed by this profile-only extension. No source fetch or model call was
made. Integration still needs governed builds and retrieval/retention evaluation;
a declared path alone does not prove that it survives an actual package budget.
