# Pension Credit foundation selection review

This is an additive source-read proposal, not a benefits decision or specialist acceptance. It selects 24 complete existing units from frozen Decision makers’ guide (DMG) chapters 77, 84 and 85. The five profiles concern Pension Credit (PC) foundations, normal-amount calculation, capital, eligibility and State Pension income in the PC assessment. No source text, machine boundary or existing concept is changed.

## Source scope

- DMG 77031 retains all six entitlement gates; 77032 and 77033 keep the absence and age routes.
- DMG 77300–77304, 77330–77334 and 77350 retain the distinction between Guarantee Credit and Savings Credit, the normal-amount scope, special-group routes, exceptions, additions, dated restrictions and the complete historical example. Historical example figures are not current rates.
- DMG 84001–84002 and 84911 keep actual and notional capital, ownership, valuation and disregards alongside deemed income. The deemed-income threshold is not presented as a universal maximum-savings rule.
- DMG 85091–85092, 85100, 85130 and 85171 retain income categories, foreign-benefit treatment, further retirement-income routes and annuity qualifications. Their direction is income assessment for PC, not independent State Pension entitlement.
- DMG 77035 and 77140 retain distinct mixed-age definitions, dates, notes and unresolved memo/statutory references.

Every profile retains explicit absent obligations for applicability, legal version, specialist review, task scope and its unresolved evidence branches. A profile activating or a required path being delivered does not close those obligations. PC abroad and care-home questions may receive the small introductory PC gateway; it cannot establish their particular rules.

## Why routing guards are required

The first compiler draft used conjunctive requirement activation but ordinary graph edges from broad topics such as capital. That would let a capital route supply PC evidence to an unrelated benefit question even though the PC requirement did not activate. The draft was not activated. Its [initial proposal](../../evaluation/manual-structure/selection-proposals/pre-reference-auxiliary-pc-core-2026-09-23/) is preserved.

Each new assertion now declares `context_guard.when_all`, containing every profile trigger. The reusable Explorer engine must satisfy this guard from directly resolved public concepts before traversing it. Merely reaching a concept or activating an assessment requirement cannot satisfy it. Assertion identities include profile identity and the complete guard, so separate reasons cannot collapse into one misleading scope.

The compiler preserves inherited concepts, assertions and requirements. Sixteen producer controls cover source identity, boundaries, scoped paths, absent obligations, distinct scopes and guard identities, unknown concepts, authority escalation, explicit registration and cross-benefit eligibility. Actual engine traversal has a separate reusable guard test suite; the Python controls do not claim to replace it.
