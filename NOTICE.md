## How to use the exemplar

- Follow the original PDF link and page locator before relying on an extract.
- Check the current publication, applicable legislation, amendments, memos
  and effective dates. A retrieval date does not establish legal currency.
- Treat extracted text as a machine transcription: reading order, tables,
  footnotes and cross-page passages may contain errors.
- Treat project-authored concepts, questions and relationships as aids to
  discovery. They are not official interpretations or decisions.
- Keep historical amendments separate from the current publication's main
  chapter files. Even a file currently linked by GOV.UK can contain historical
  or transitional provisions.

This is not legal, financial or benefits advice and does not determine
eligibility, entitlement or an award. It contains no claimant case data and
asks for no personal information. The intended use is professional
discussion, source discovery and evaluation of evidence retrieval.
