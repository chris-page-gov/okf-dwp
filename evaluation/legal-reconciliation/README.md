# Legal reference evaluation

[Beginner explanation and demonstration](../../docs/legal-reconciliation.md)

- [Coverage](coverage.json): every candidate page, request outcome and unknown.
- [Citation census](citations.json): exact literals, offsets, hashes, question
  occurrence IDs, selected identity proposals and unresolved locators.
- [Navigation assertions](assertions.json): only `dcterms:references`, with frozen
  page and official metadata provenance. No legal applicability assertions.
- [Observed effects metadata](effects.json): source-native effect identifiers and
  attributes; not a complete amendment chain or target-specific applicability.
- [Tribunal discovery](tribunal-discovery.json): actual queries, limits and selected
  decision metadata. No keyword-derived applicability or judgment bodies.

All outputs are generated by `scripts/build_legal_reconciliation.py` and checked
offline with `--check`. No legal acceptance is inferred from a passing technical
check. The separate independent review remains pending.
