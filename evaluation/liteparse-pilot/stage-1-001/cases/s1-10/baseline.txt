A2003 “Appropriate Office” means1


1. an office of DWP or


2. any other place designated by the Secretary of State in relation to any case or class of case as a place
to, or at which, any claim, notice, document, evidence or other information may be sent, delivered or
received, including a postal address specified for that purpose or


3. in the case of a person who is authorised or required to use an electronic communication for any
purpose, an address to which such communications may be sent in accordance with prescribed

regulations2.


Note: Where an individual office or address has been specified e.g. for PIP then this is where
correspondence must be sent.
