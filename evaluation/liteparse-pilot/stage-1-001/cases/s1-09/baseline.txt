Basic conditions

E1013 The basic conditions1 are met if the person


1. is at least 18 years old (see E1024)


2. has not reached the qualifying age for SPC (see E1071)


3. is in GB (see E1091)


4. is not receiving education (see E1101) and


5. has accepted a claimant commitment (see E1121).


                                                                                       1 WR Act 12, s 4(1)



Financial conditions

E1014 The financial conditions


1. for a single claimant1 are that the claimant’s


      1.1 capital does not exceed the prescribed amount and


      1.2 income is such that if the claimant were entitled to UC, the amount payable would not be less
      than any prescribed minimum or


2. for joint claimants2 are that their combined


      2.1 capital is not greater than the prescribed amount and


      2.2 income is such that if they were entitled to UC, the amount payable would not be less that any
      prescribed minimum.
